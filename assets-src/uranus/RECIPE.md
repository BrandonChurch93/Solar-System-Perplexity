# Uranus asset recipes

Every file under `public/assets/uranus/full/` has a twin under `public/assets/uranus/web/` made by the same script in the same run. Work files live under `out/work/u/` (not in git). All scripts run from the repo root. Shared geometry of the hold frame (camera, ellipsoid, pixel rays, tone curve) is in `assets-src/uranus/geo.py`.

Input made once for several assets: `out/work/u/hero-2x.png`, the approved hero at twice its size.
- Tool: `python3 tools/ml/upscale.py --in references/approved/uranus-hero.png --out out/work/u/hero-2x.png --width 5632` (the repo's upscaler, see `tools/ml/MODELS.md`). No prompt.

## wrap-color.jpg, wrap-cloud.png, wrap-shadow.png, light.bin, shade.bin

- Output: `public/assets/uranus/<set>/wrap-color.jpg` (8192 x 4096 full, 4096 x 2048 web), `public/assets/uranus/<set>/wrap-cloud.png` (cloud alpha in red, cloud shadow in green, same sizes), `public/assets/uranus/<set>/light.bin` (513 x 128 x 4 float: light by sun cosine and view cosine), `public/assets/uranus/<set>/shade.bin` (512 x 256 x 4 float: world-fixed light map).
- Output: `public/assets/uranus/<set>/wrap-shadow.png` (8192 x 4096 full, 4096 x 2048 web, one grey channel). Source: the green channel of `public/assets/uranus/<set>/wrap-cloud.png` of the same set, bit for bit. Tool: `python3 assets-src/uranus/place/pack.py shadow` (PIL only, writes both sets, about 20 seconds). No prompt, no generation, no takes: the one run is kept. Why: the globe loads the cloud and its shadow as two textures of one channel each, which saves 89.5 MB of the set's memory. Check: `python3 -c "from PIL import Image; import numpy as np; a=np.asarray(Image.open('public/assets/uranus/full/wrap-cloud.png'))[...,1]; b=np.asarray(Image.open('public/assets/uranus/full/wrap-shadow.png')); print(int((a ^ b).max()))"` prints 0.
- Tool: numpy, OpenCV and SciPy only, no model. Run in this order:
  `python3 assets-src/uranus/wrap/stage_a.py`, `stage_a2.py`, `stage_b.py`, `stage_c.py`, `stage_d.py`, `stage_e.py`, `stage_f.py`, `stage_g.py`.
- Inputs: `out/work/u/hero-2x.png`, `references/approved/uranus-hero.png`.
- Prompt: none.
- What each stage does:
  - `stage_a.py`: separates the bright cloud flecks and their bluish shadows from the globe (red channel against a median, inpainted base), fits the 513 knot light curve by sun cosine, writes `curve.json` with the sun direction, the band axis and the globe quaternion.
  - `stage_a2.py`: fits the light as a table over sun cosine and view cosine (view cosine over 0.05).
  - `stage_b.py`: unprojects the base image through the fitted ellipsoid to an equirectangular map in the band-axis frame. Albedo = hero / light. Fades the hero's pixels out between view cosine 0.34 and 0.14.
  - `stage_c.py`: zonal colour profile by latitude from the fully seen texels, mirrored north of 44 degrees; the unseen side is the zonal profile times eight octaves of streak noise stretched along the bands (warped 3D lattice noise sampled on the sphere, so there is no seam and no pinched pole) plus two tint fields. Divides the seen albedo by the low-pass (2.5 degrees) of albedo over fill and ships that low-pass as `shade.bin`, so the broad light structure of the hero stays fixed in space while the fine albedo turns. Night-side albedo is desaturated to 15 percent chroma and kept at 30 percent. Cloud map: the hero's flecks, plus a mirrored copy on the unseen side.
  - `stage_d.py`: writes both sets.
  - `stage_e.py`: replaces the light table rows below view cosine 0.2 by direct medians of hero over shipped albedo times shade, in bins across the last 6 percent of the disc radius.
  - `stage_f.py` (Phase 6 polish item 7, single white specks): the hero's small cloud dots (under 24 px at twice the hero's size) stayed in the albedo, clipped at 1.8, one or two texels wide, and came out as hard cyan points on the sunlit globe and as beads in the dive. In the seen part of the map it finds blobs whose red albedo is over 1.3 times the 31 texel median (peak over 1.5, at most 400 texels, 8 texels clear of the separated clouds: 284 dots, 9225 texels), rebuilds their excess from the unclipped `map-albedo.npy`, blurs it by 1.8 texels and scales green and blue by 0.68 (measured against the hero at the final tier: lit dots peak film over hero 1.05 1.03 0.98, before 1.48 1.88 1.48). Writes `out/work/u/wrap/wrap-albedo-f.npy` (the input of `dive/patches.py`), `wrap-color-f.png` and `wrap-color.jpg` in both sets. 67785 texels of the map change. Run after `stage_e.py`; `stage_e.py` is not run again after it.
  - `stage_g.py` (Phase 6 polish item 7, night limb; Mail 79, the departure): `stage_c.py` let the shade map return to 1 between hold view cosine 0.10 and 0.04, which drew a bright rim on the night limb of the hold and left the far side beside the hero's shadow unshaded. It keeps the shade where the blurred weight is over 0.6 (largest change there 2e-7), continues it outward with the value of the nearest such texel (distance transform, wrapped in longitude, blur 2 texels) and fades it to 1 between 45 and 80 degrees behind the hold's limb. Writes `out/work/u/wrap/shade-g.npy` and `shade.bin` in both sets.
- Take kept: the current run (deterministic, seeds fixed in the scripts).
- Checks: `python3 assets-src/uranus/check/disc.py STILL` (colour by cell within 4 percent of the hero, detail bands 0.80 to 0.99 of the hero's with correlation 0.74 to 1.00 at the review tier), `check/limb.py`, `check/crops.py`.

## halo.bin

- Output: `halo.bin`, 64 x 64 x 4 float: glow outside the disc by impact parameter 1.0 to 1.3 (square-root rows) and sun cosine at the grazing point.
- Tool: `python3 assets-src/uranus/halo/halo.py`. No model, no prompt.
- Input: `references/approved/uranus-hero.png`, `out/work/u/wrap/curve.json`.
- What it does: medians of the hero outside the fitted ellipsoid minus the far background (percentile 50 on the lit side inside 1.04, percentile 35 elsewhere, because stars lift the median), made monotone outward, the first rows on the night side held from 1.0055 so the hero's blurred disc edge is not counted twice.
- Checks: `check/limb.py STILL angles` beside the hero.
- Phase 6 polish item 7 ("the limb glow a hair bright and wide"): `python3 assets-src/uranus/halo/refit.py STILL [STILL2]` after `halo.py`. It measures the hero and a final tier still of frame 4035 in the table's own cells (scene light by sun cosine and impact parameter, the film undrifted with `geo.undrift`), takes the relative residual (median 5 x 3 cells, Gaussian 3 x 1.5 cells, mean of the channels), applies it from 1.004 to 1.012 radii outward within -30 and +15 percent and makes the rows monotone again; a second still, rendered with the table of the first pass, gives a second pass. Still used: the final tier still of 4035 of commit 8d48b37 (the tree before item 7), one pass. A second pass was measured and dropped: under 3 percent at the sunward limb, and near the terminator it followed the noise. Writes `out/work/u/halo/halo-lut-h.npy` and `halo.bin` in both sets.

## rings.bin

- Output: `rings.bin`: 81 ring lines as sampled 3D curves with amplitude (13 closed rings, 68 open arcs, 12,779 samples).
- Tool: `python3 assets-src/uranus/rings/trace.py` then `python3 assets-src/uranus/rings/build.py`. No model, no prompt.
- Input: `references/approved/uranus-hero.png`.
- What it does: `trace.py` unwraps the hero's residual (hero minus an oriented median that removes stars) in the fitted ring plane and tracks ridges. `build.py` gives each track of 25 degrees or more its own fitted plane and closes it into a ring with a floor amplitude on the unseen side; shorter tracks stay open arcs; amplitudes over 0.3 are halved above the knee. `rings/gain.json` must not exist (an abandoned calibration reads it).
- Checks: crops `ul`, `top`, `right`, `ll`, `lr` of `check/crops.py`.

### Asset `dive-detail-2` (generated, Phase 6 polish item 3, 1 take of 6 used, take 1 kept)

- Why: at the judged dive frame 4137 one screen pixel is 41 to 74 millidegrees, the size of one pixel of `dive-take1.png` (39 millidegrees). The take was shown at its own pixel scale, where it carries a watery micro texture, and under it the wrap's own pixel grain: the critic's soft, grainy surface. - Output: `out/work/u/gen/dive-take1-r.png` (4096 x 4096), written by `python3 assets-src/uranus/dive/refine.py` from nine generated tiles `out/work/u/gen2/r1-XY.png` (2048 x 2048 each). Not shipped as a file: it ships inside the four patches.
- Tool and model: the image generation tool, model `nano_banana_pro`, aspect 1:1, one input image per tile.
- Inputs: nine windows of `out/work/u/gen/dive-take1.png`, 1024 px wide at a stride of 512 px (x and y offsets 0, 512, 1024), each enlarged to 2048 px with Lanczos: `out/work/u/gen2/in-XY.png` (X the column, Y the row).
- Prompt, exact, the same for every tile: "Image 1 is a blurry, low-resolution enlargement of a straight-down satellite photograph of the cloud tops of the ice giant planet Uranus: a pale cyan cloud deck with soft white cloud streaks, swirls and clumps. - `refine.py`: each tile is compared with its input by phase correlation (measured shifts 0.00 to 0.03 px, so no tile is moved), its tones wider than 20 px are replaced by the input's (so tiles agree and the take keeps its forms), bright and dark dots shorter than 9 px in every direction are taken out (line openings and closings in eight directions, floor 0.05 in log light), and the tiles are joined with raised cosine weights over their half overlaps.

Source: plate 1 and its tops map as `layback.py` baked them, no generated image, code only (takes of the tops: 2, take 2 kept, see below). It was packed by `python3 assets-src/uranus/place/pack.py plate` from the `place-1.jpg` (4096 px) and the `place-tops.png` (2048 px) of commit b0c9e2b0, not baked again: the same forms at half the size, for the memory limit (the set was 1512.5 MB of 1400).
- Inputs: the deck's own files and tables (`deck-color.jpg`, `deck-near.jpg`, `deck-far.jpg`, `src/stops/uranus/deck.json`, `deck-near.json`), `wrap-color.jpg`, `patch-1.jpg` to `patch-4.jpg` of commit cf300549, `assets-src/uranus/dive/spot.json`.
- What it does:
  - Lays the ground of the close view back onto the globe: four nested gnomonic plates centred 1 unit ahead of the deck's up point (1 unit is 0.015133 R), half tangent 0.28 halved per level, holding the albedo the deck shader shades, sampled through pyramids at the level the plate texel needs.
  - Makes the place a round storm (`storm`): the fan the close picture sees is kept, the ring round it is filled from the same picture in polar form (5 ring bands, each turned by its own angle, mirrored at the fan's sides), with a core of 2.6 to 4.2 units kept as the deck shades it.
  - Repaint by code (`detail`): band limited streak noise along the arcs of the storm, an octave only where its wavelength is at least 6 to 10 texels of the plate (so no plate holds grain at the pixel scale) and under the pixel footprint of the handover cameras (so the close view never shows it), luma gain capped.
  - Each plate's middle is replaced by the next finer plate brought down, so a level low passed equals its parent.
  - `place-field.bin`: rgb is the colour match (low pass of the globe's albedo at the place over low pass of plate 1, 2 degrees), a is a soft irregular round mask, 6 to 9 degrees from the plate centre. The globe shader draws `mix(globe, plate * match, mask)`.
  - The alpha of `place-1.png` (`tops`): the share of high, sunlit cloud tops in a texel, in the square of plate 1. The luma of plate 1, blurred by 5 texels, times a smooth gain map, through the soft threshold `luma` (1.25 to 3.0), then the area mean to 2048 px. The gain (512 px) is 1 in the fan the close picture sees and `fill` 0.8 round it, plus `streak` (0.6, a gaussian of 1.6 units at the bright streak of the close picture), plus the `arcs` (angle from and to in degrees from the view direction of T, radius from and to in units, width, gain; each a gaussian across, tapered at its ends, times a low noise between 0.55 and 1), and falls to zero between 9.5 and 12 units from the up point. The forms are the plate's own bright fibres; the gain only says where the air is high. The shader adds `white * tops * smoothstep(sun)` of `look.place.tops` on the globe side of the dive and the rise.
- Takes of the tops (code only): 1 (a threshold in the shader on the plate luma times a marbled gain, with a second map of the mean cover for far views) rejected: white blobs with hard outlines and dark hairlines, sand at the edges; 2 kept: one filtered map, smooth gain.
