# Jupiter assets: recipes

All scripts run from the repo root. Wrap scripts need `PYTHONPATH=assets-src/jupiter/wrap`, the sky script `PYTHONPATH=assets-src/jupiter/sky`. Takes and inputs live under `out/work/jupiter/` (not in git). Image tool: `generate_image` of the agent runner. Models by tool id: `seedream_5` (2816 x 1584, edits stay pixel aligned), `nano_banana_pro` (2752 x 1536, stretches 0.8 percent vertically).

## `public/assets/jupiter/<set>/wrap-color.jpg` (full 8192 x 4096, web 4096 x 2048)

Tone coded albedo (sRGB of the filmic curve), decoded in `src/stops/jupiter/globe.ts`.

1. `python3 tools/ml/upscale.py` on `references/approved/jupiter-hero.png` gives `hero2x.png` (5632 x 3168).
2. Io and its shadow painted out (`wrap/nomoon.py`). Tool `nano_banana_pro`, 16:9, input `gen/io_crop_in.jpg` (a 1792 x 1008 crop of the hero round Io). Prompt: "Edit this image: remove the small yellow moon and its black oval shadow completely. Fill the place where they were with Jupiter's cloud bands continuing naturally from the surroundings: the amber and rust turbulent belt continues across where the moon was, and the pale cream zone below it continues across where the shadow was. Match the colors, lighting, softness and painterly level of detail of the surrounding clouds exactly. Keep every other part of the image exactly as it is: same framing, same position of every cloud feature, same dark night side on the right. No moon, no shadow, no text, no border. Wide 16:9 image." Two takes, one per model. Kept `gen/ioremove_nbp.png` (natural fill), rejected `gen/ioremove_sd5.png` (bland fill). The take is resized to 1792 x 1000 and reflect padded 4 px top and bottom to undo the stretch, kept only inside the moon and shadow mask, low frequencies matched to the hero. Output `hero2x-nomoon.png`.
3. Light fit `wrap/delight.py`: seen = albedo(lat) x curve(ndl) x limb(mu), alternating medians. Writes `src/stops/jupiter/curve.json`, `limb.json`.
4. `wrap/unproject.py`: delit hero to the equirect (`eq8k-color.png`, weight = smoothstep(0.2, 0.42, mu) x smoothstep(0.035, 0.11, curve G)). Hero pixels cover lon -62 to +36, lat +-65.
5. Far side, `wrap/guide.py` and `wrap/tiles.py init | prep NAME | place NAME TAKE`. The guide is the hero's zonal mean color per latitude (flat stripes). Three tiles of 3200 x 1800 px at 8k (lat +-39.55), x0 490 (t1), 3860 (t3), 6271 (t2), each sent at 2816 x 1584 with finished pixels at one or both sides. Tool `seedream_5`, 16:9, input `gen/{t1,t3,t2}_in.jpg`, take 1 kept for each (bands continue at the right height, detail in the hero's style).
   - t1 and t3: "Edit this image. It is a flat cylindrical (equirectangular) texture map of Jupiter's cloud bands. The {right|left} quarter is finished: detailed, turbulent, painted clouds. The rest is only flat blurred color stripes. Paint the striped part to the same finish: continue every band from the finished part straight to the {left|right} edge at exactly the same height, color and brightness, and fill it with sharp, richly detailed turbulence: curling filaments, folded and scalloped edges between bands, chains of small eddies, a few white ovals, all different from each other. Do not change the finished part. Bands run perfectly horizontal across the full width. Flat even lighting, no shading, no vignette. No sphere, no curvature, no limb, no black space, no stars, no new red spot, no moon, no text, no border. Wide 16:9 image."
   - t2: the same text with "The left quarter and the right quarter are finished: detailed, turbulent, painted clouds. The middle is only flat blurred color stripes. Paint the striped middle to the same finish: join every band of the left part to the same band of the right part" and "no red spot".
   - Placement: low frequencies locked to the canvas (sigma 80, 0.85), feathered over 140 to 520 px. Seam at lon 180 measured 1.98 mean code values against 1.75 between neighbours.
   - Rejected methods: free repaint (band heights reinvented), `nano_banana_pro` on a guide (keeps the guide's smears), a quilt of hero pixels (`wrap/quilt.py`, repeats every 83 deg).
6. Polar caps on an azimuthal equidistant projection (60 deg, 2048 px), `tiles.py prep n|s`, `place n|s`. Tool `seedream_5`, 1:1, input `gen/{n,s}_in.jpg`, take 1 kept for each. Prompt: "Edit this image. It is a flat top-down polar map of Jupiter's cloud deck: the pole is at the exact center and the cloud bands are concentric circular rings around it. The outer ring and the wedge at the {bottom|top} are finished: detailed, turbulent, painted clouds. The rest is only flat blurred color rings and a flat disc in the middle. Paint all of the unfinished area to the same finish and in the same style as the finished wedge: a chaotic mottled field of many small cyclones, tiny round pale storms, folded filaments and curdled turbulence in grey-brown, tan, olive and slate blue, arranged in faint concentric rings, growing darker and finer toward the pole at the center, with a loose cluster of small round cyclones at the very center. Keep the ring colors and brightness of the draft. Join seamlessly to the finished parts and do not change them. Flat even lighting, no shading, no vignette, no sphere, no limb, no black space, no stars, no large red spot, no moon, no text, no border. Square 1:1 image."
7. `wrap/write.py`: north cap chroma pulled 80 percent toward the chroma of lat 56 to 62 above lat 58 to 72 (the painted cap came out teal against the hero's tan limb, luminance kept), then JPEG 94 (full) and 90 (web, area resize).

Checks: no seam at lon 180, no pinch at the poles (polar projection), hero side equals the hero under the fitted light (`npm run compare -- --stop jupiter --view hold`), no moon, no shadow, no stars, no text.
