- Exact steps and parameters:
  1. Decode `references/cache/sky/milkyway_2020_8k_gal.exr` into linear float32 RGB via `pyvips`.
  2. Strip-wise residual star and shot-noise removal (`clean_stage` in `tools/assets/sky.py`): horizontal wrap-padded `3x3` and `5x5` median filters clamp positive point spikes above `1.08x` (`3x3`) and `1.16x` (`5x5`) local median luminance, blended 50/50 with the `3x3` median; two multi-scale compact-cluster suppression passes at `1/2` scale (`sigma = 3.5`, ratio threshold `1.58`) and `1/4` scale (`sigma = 4.5`, ratio threshold `1.65`) dissolve unresolved cluster cores without touching dark dust lanes.
  3. Four-scale starless glow pyramid and dust-envelope extraction: assemble `f1` (`sigma = 1.65 px` at 8K inside the dense galactic spine `|b| < 11 deg`), `f2` (`sigma = 5.40 px` across `|b| < 16.5 deg`), `f3` (`1/4` scale `sigma = 5.0` with high-latitude 70th-percentile zonal clamping above `|b| > 10.5 deg`), and `f4` (`1/8` scale `sigma = 10.5` with 45th-percentile zonal clamping to dissolve off-plane extragalactic blobs), plus polar-cap convergence (`|b| > 80 deg`) to the pole mean. Compute the upper star-cloud envelope `env` directly from `gal` via `1/4`-scale `27x27` elliptical morphological closing and `49x49` Gaussian blur (`sigma = 12.0`).
  4. Spherical coordinate rotation (`rotate_stage`): map the galactic north pole to `normalize(0.45, 0.82, 0.35)` with azimuthal phase `gamma = 0.28 rad` using spherical pole-flipped padding and `cv2.INTER_CUBIC` remapping. This tilts the Milky Way 34.8 degrees diagonally across the equirectangular frame, aligns the glow band with the procedural star-point plane in `src/space/sky.ts`, places the wrap seam in a quiet outer arm, and places both output poles in smooth high-latitude sky (`|b| = 55.2 deg`).
  5. Linear tone map, dust-lane contrast, and color grade (`grade_stage`, `take-05-sculpted`): subtract dark floor `0.00038`, compute dust transmission `T = (luma - floor + 1e-4) / (env - floor + 1e-4)` raised to `dust = 1.64` inside the band, lift outer arms by `arm_boost = 1.46` outside the bulge, apply smooth shadow toe `sig^2 / (sig + 0.00085)`, exposure `7.4`, gamma `0.87`, and Reinhard shoulder `0.75`. Tint outer faint haze cool slate-cerulean `cool = (0.48, 0.87, 1.48)`, mid-clouds `(0.86, 0.98, 1.18)`, bulge and bright core golden-ochre `warm = (1.42, 1.03, 0.67)` (`warm_start = 0.060`, `warm_span = 0.24`), and dust-lane edges cool indigo `dusk = (0.56, 0.76, 1.02)` over linear deep-space black floor `bg = (0.0021, 0.0034, 0.0054)` (which renders to sRGB `[2, 3, 5]` after the filmic curve in `src/post/chain.ts`, matching the approved hero skies).
  6. Convert linear float32 to sRGB, apply seeded triangular PDF dithering (`seed = 20261119` for `full`, `seed + 1` for `web` after wrap-padded `cv2.INTER_AREA` downsample from float32), and encode JPEG at `quality = 95`, `subsampling = 0` (4:4:4 chroma), `optimize = True`.
- Take kept: `take-05-sculpted` out of 5 takes (`take-01-subtle`, `take-02-pushed`, `take-03-warm-heavy`, `take-04-wide-haze`, `take-05-sculpted`; rejected takes left under `out/work/sky/takes/`). `take-01-subtle` left the outer arms too dim; `take-02-pushed` lifted the dark space background too bright blue; `take-03-warm-heavy` lost the cool cerulean outer starlight seen in `neptune-hero.png` and `uranus-hero.png`; `take-04-wide-haze` cast too broad a diffuse band halo across mid-latitudes; `take-05-sculpted` matches the deep midnight space floor, cool blue outer filaments, dense dark dust rifts, and warm golden bulge of the approved hero frames.

- Exact steps in `tools/assets/skyband.py`:
  1. Each take is enlarged by 4/3 with Lanczos to `3755 x 2112`, and cut to 2048 rows centered on its band (the brightness weighted mean row of a blurred copy).
  2. Left to right leveling: the mean of the central 744 rows per column, blurred with sigma 425 px, is brought to the mean of all takes with a gain held between 0.62 and 1.6, applied with a Gaussian weight across the band so the dark sky above and below is untouched.
  3. The four takes are joined in a ring. Each joint overlaps about 1707 px; the cut follows the path of least difference through the overlap (dynamic programming over the summed color difference, kept 260 px away from the overlap's edges) and is feathered over 36 px. The last take joins the first the same way, so the strip wraps.
  4. sRGB, seeded triangular dither (`seed`), JPEG quality 93 at 4:4:4. The web file is an area downsample of the float image, dithered with `seed + 1`.
