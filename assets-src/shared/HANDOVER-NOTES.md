### 13:10 to 13:50, a fault of E2's pass at the horizon, and its cure (the limb hold)
- Seen on dive frames 2445 to 2453 and growing through the rise from 2690: a dark line along the horizon, between the ground and the haze of the picture (8 pixels high at 2447).
- Why. The camera of the place window is above the twin, so its limb lies beyond the horizon of the picture. A sky ray between the two was below the twin's horizon, so the pass called it unseen (weight 0) and the globe's own sky showed: dark, with stars, where the close view has its bright dust haze. The ground between the twin's horizon and the limb (one to a few pixels, foreshortened) had no weight either.
- The picture's horizon itself (dumps `out/work/e4/matte/f2454-dump-image.png`, `-alpha.png`): haze meets haze near window row 540, no relief stands above the horizon row, the far mesas start 20 to 55 texels below it. So the row of the horizon may be reused freely.
- The cure, in `src/core/place.ts` (LAY and its mirror `placeUv`), three parts, all off when the reach is 0:
  1. Sky lift. `gap` = the camera's dip minus the twin's dip in the direction of the pixel (`uLimb`: camera up minus twin up, and the difference of the two sines). A sky pixel `own` above the limb looks the picture up `gap * (1 - smoothstep(0, 1, own / max(reach, 2 |gap|)))` higher along the twin's up: at the limb it reads the picture's horizon, at the reach it reads where the twin saw it. Symmetric: a camera below the twin squeezes. The weight of a sky pixel is then whole (`honest` 1).
  2. Ground clamp and hold band. A hit point beyond the twin's horizon looks up the horizon row of the picture at its own azimuth. `hold = 1 - smoothstep(limbHoldDeg[0], limbHoldDeg[1], angle under the limb)` and the weight is at least place weight times the edges times `hold`, so the band under the limb keeps the picture where `seen` and `maxEnlarge` would drop it.
  3. Derivatives. A lifted sky pixel and a clamped ground pixel take the size of a pixel in the picture from the algebra of a direction (`placeSkyGrad` is the mirror), not from `dFdx` and `dFdy`. Why: on the limb a 2 by 2 block holds sky and ground, the picture coordinate jumps inside it (16 texels at 2447), the smaller singular value of that false Jacobian falls under the `maxEnlarge` limit and the pixel loses its weight. Seen as single dark pixels at the stair corners of the limb in the dive (2447: 1421,310 and 1787,314) and as a dashed dark line in the rise (2696, x 1485 to 1900).
- Numbers: `config.handover.place.limbReachDeg` 0 (off film wide) and `limbHoldDeg` [0.25, 0.75]; a stop turns it on per kind with `handover: { dive: { place: { limbReachDeg: 8 } } }` and may give its own `limbHoldDeg`. Mars has 8 on both kinds, from `src/stops/mars/place.json` with a `limbNote`. Harness option `limb=DEG` on the diag string overrides the stop.
- Why off by default: a body without air has stars down to its horizon in both views, so there is nothing dark in the gap, and the lift would move the picture's stars against the globe's own (double stars near the limb). It is for a close view whose horizon carries a haze the globe side does not draw.

## E3 as built (11:30), the generic path of a stop that opted in

- Two schedules. `share` (trapeze of the time between the far end and T with `rise`, `fall`) drives log height and the field of view. `turn` (trapeze with `turnRise`, `turnFall`) drives the direction from the place and the attitude. The velocity ramps of the trapeze are smoothsteps. Generic numbers: rise 0.2, fall 0.3, turnRise 0.2, turnFall 0.6 for both kinds.
- The low end is not the twin of the close pose at the sample time but `twin(coast(kind, time))`: the close pose at T carried on with its own velocity and spin, the reach saturating over `coastFrames` (8, tanh). At T it is the twin with the close side's speed, turn rate and climb (tested to 1e-3).
- Level glide (`level: true`, the generic default). The far pose is swung about the vertical of the place into the upright plane that holds the close side's heading (`swing`), the path is flown in that plane, and the swing is given back as the camera gets far. So near the ground the camera flies straight along the heading, the horizon is level whenever it is a line in the frame, and the place picture is seen from the twin's own side.
  - Geometry: looking at the place from elevation e and off plane azimuth phi with the picture upright, the horizon tilts by atan(tan(phi) / sin(e)). Level needs phi = 0 and no twist.

- The picture is the close side at T, whole: `director.shotAt(frameOfT, T, 'terrain')`, the world and near scenes of the stop's set, `post.vignette` 0 so no set undoes the lens in it. Camera = the close camera of T with a wider field (`placeCamera`: `setViewOffset`, same vfov). Two float targets, one cleared black (K) and one white (W); packed into one half float picture with mip levels: RGB = K, A = 1 minus the mean of W minus K (so exactly 1 where the set is opaque, 0 where only sky shows). `matte: 'alpha'` takes the set's own alpha from one render.
- Layout (`placeLayout`): `scale` whole texels per frame pixel (2; drops until the edge fits `min(place.maxEdge, device limit)`), margins per side from `valid: [left, right, bottom, top]` as shares of the frame (rounded up, at least 1 pixel) or `margin / 2` on every side; a half texel shift for even scales so texel centres sit on frame pixel centres. `place.maxShare` (0.5) bounds two opposite margins together; `caps.needs` asks for the buffer of that bound (2882 for the master format, inside the recorded 8192). Mars: 4496 x 2668.
- While the picture is drawn: `gradUniform` times scale and `setFine(scale)` (so `uFine` = scale and `uBias` = log2(scale) in the relief shader: mask and alpha lookups take the same mip level as in the frame, apron alpha gradients are per frame pixel), `view.place` true, `view.placeScale` the scale, `view.format` the frame format times scale. A set whose shader uses screen derivatives or implicit mip levels must scale them by `fineUniform` and `biasUniform` from `src/diagnostics/diag.ts` (both 1 and 0 outside the place picture).
- The pass (`Place.render`, after world, near and belt, before `over` and the cover): per pixel a ray against the unit sphere of the stop in camera relative coordinates; a hit projects the sphere point into the twin, a miss projects its direction as a point at infinity. Out = under * (1 - k) + k * (sky * (1 - A) + RGB * lens undo at this pixel). The sky is a copy of the sample target taken right after the sky and dust are drawn (`keepSky`); in the place window the world is drawn without the early pass.
- k = place weight * front * edge feather (inside the margin: `place.edge` of it) * `smoothstep(0, place.seen, honest)` * the enlargement limit (`place.maxEnlarge`, `place.enlargeSoft`). honest for a hit = (n . (twin - P)) / (n . (camera - P)) * height ratio: 0 at the twin's horizon, 1 at T everywhere; for a miss = (sin elevation seen from the twin + sin dip of the twin) / (the same for the camera).
- Sampling: `textureGrad` with the screen derivatives of the picture coordinate times `grad / scale`, capped at `place.maxGradTexels`. At T that is one texel at a texel centre: the close side's own pixel.
- The twin: `StopPlan.twin(local, time)`, a similarity from the close scene onto the unit sphere. Up of the close scene in terrain frame coordinates a = (0, cos tilt, sin tilt), tilt = horizonDeg + dipDeg, rolled by rollDeg; ground sphere radius rho = h0 / (1 / cos(dip) - 1), h0 = `terrain.ground ?? motion.terrainGround`. `twinFrame(time)` = {rotation: turn(time) * terrainFrame * R^-1 (R takes y to a), origin: the turned spot * (1 + h0 / rho), scale: 1 / rho}. A plan may override `twin` (Earth, Neptune do); the place, the pass, the globe camera at T and the sky carry all go through `plan.twin` only.
- The globe side of a stop that opted in (moves.ts): `descent(time)` and `ascent(time)` = `glide(far = hold pose or the pose after the rise, low = twin(close pose(time)), share)`, share = `trapeze` of the time between the far end and T (`descent.rise`, `descent.fall`). The glide keeps the look target (the point the twin looks at on the ground, at most `descent.targetReach` of the way to its horizon) and mixes log height, direction from the target and the rotation against the look frame. After T (dive) and before T (rise) the globe pose is the twin of the close pose, so the camera is C1 at T. E3 replaces this curve by a rate limited one.
- The sky through T (film.ts, globe side only): `Shot.skyShare` = `skyWeight` (0 until `skyFrames` before T, 1 at T); the glow is mixed to `terrainSkyGlow` by it, the skyLow factor fades out by it, both sky patches are passed with weights (1 - share) and share; the sky turn slerps `sky.orbits` to `sky.turns` by `skyTurnWeight` (0 at the far end of the segment, 1 at T, smootherstep); the sky quaternion is `slerp(1, E, turnWeight) * camera`, E = Qclose_world(tc) * Qtwin_world(tc)^-1 with tc the time clamped to the place window. At T the sky quaternion, the turn, the exposure and the glow are equal on both sides (tested).
- Near plane of the globe side of a stop that opted in: `min(0.0004, 0.2 * height)` as the floor, so the twin at 0.001 radii does not clip the ground.

- Opt in: `handover: {}` in the plan's params (Mars: `src/stops/mars/plan.ts`, with `place.json`). Per kind: `at`, `dissolveFrames`, `placeFrames`, `skyFrames`, `live`, `picture`, `place: {scale, margin, valid, matte, lens}`.

- MAIL 222, tall things: (a) the set reads `view.place === true` in `update` and hides what must not ride in the picture (or keeps such things out of `world` and `near`); (b) the set gives `over: THREE.Object3D` in its `StopSet`; the engine keeps it at the set's position and rotation and draws it after the pass on both sides and in raw views, against the depth buffer as it stands, never into the picture. The stop draws the same thing with the same light on the close side, so E4's side difference counts it.
- No picture at all: `handover: { rise: { picture: false } }`. The sides still swap at T, the sky is still carried, no place state is made. For a stop whose set is the same true 3D object on both sides (the Sun asked). `placeFrames: 0` keeps the picture in the dissolve frames only.
- The field of the picture: `place.valid` = [left, right, bottom, top] margins as shares of the frame, the feather lies inside them (Mercury asked; `[0, 0, 0, 0]` gives one pixel). `place.lens` scales the lens undo of the pass (1 = what sets do).
- Own twin: override `twin(local, time)` in the plan class.
- The picture's broad tone lent by the globe near its sides (17:50): `handover: { dive: { place: { toneIn: [left, right, bottom, top] } }, rise: { place: { toneIn: [left, right, bottom, top] } } }`. Each number is the reach of that side's feather as a share of the picture once the weight is 0 (0 to 4; at 0.5 and above the feathers of two sides meet, at 2 the whole tone is the globe's a few frames from T). Absent or all 0: off, the pass and its shader are the ones they were. Mars sets `[2, 2, 2, 2]` on both. It cures a picture that stands on the globe as a lighter or darker slab; it does nothing for a side that shows as a line of detail (that is edgeIn) or for ground that is not the same ground (that is the plates). Film wide numbers in `handover.place`: `tonePower` 0.5, `toneReach` 0.045 (the blur, of the frame's height), `toneCell` 8, `toneLandDeg` [1, 6]. Harness: `--diag "beauty/tone=2,2,2,2"` tries it on any frame of a stop without touching its plan, `tone=0,0,0,0` turns it off.
- Harness options on the diag string (`npm run still -- --diag "beauty/side=orbit/noplace"`): `side=orbit|terrain`, `shut` (every sample at the shutter middle), `t=SECONDS`, `noplace`, `mask` (the pass weight as grey), `picture` (the whole picture with its margins). File names carry the options.

- `StopPlan.risePose` (a property, the pose at the end of the rise) is renamed `riseEnd`, because the contract of E1 needs the method `risePose(time, view)`. One line outside my folders follows it: `src/stops/earth/plan.ts:91`. Told to /root with M1.

With `at` the share of the segment, `centreFrame = floor((start + at * length) * fps)`, the dissolve frames are `first = centreFrame - floor((n - 1) / 2)` to `last = first + n - 1`, and `T = ((first + last) / 2 + shutterFraction / 2) / fps`. For `n = 1` the draft sample of the dissolve frame lies exactly at T.
- The dissolve weight is constant inside a frame (taken from the frame number), so both sides share every part of that frame's shutter: `w = smooth((frame - first + 1) / (n + 1))` in a dive, `1 - that` in a rise. At the draft tier (one sample at s 0.5, threshold 0.472) the dive swaps to the close side at the first frame whose weight is above 0.472.
- The place window: dive `placeFirstFrame = firstFrame - placeFrames`, `placeLastFrame = lastFrame`; rise `placeFirstFrame = firstFrame`, `placeLastFrame = lastFrame + placeFrames`. `placeWeight` is continuous in time: dive 0 at `placeFirstFrame / fps` to 1 at `firstFrame / fps`, then 1; rise 1 until `(lastFrame + 1) / fps`, 0 at `(placeLastFrame + 1) / fps`.
- A stop that did not opt in: `handoverOf` null, `placeWeight` 0, `sideAt` is today's test (dive: terrain when `time >= centre`; rise: terrain when `time < centre`, centre = `coverWindow(seg).centerFrame / fps`).

- Harness hooks live in my folders: `src/capture/frame.ts` and `src/main.ts` are not mine, so options ride on the `diag` string that `setDiagnostic` already passes to the engine.

## The contract of E1, for the stop builders

- Opt in: `MoveParams.handover` in the stop's `params.json` (or its plan). Its presence, even `{}`, opts in both the dive and the rise. `handover.dive` and `handover.rise` may override `at`, `dissolveFrames`, `placeFrames` of `config.handover`.
- `plan.divePose(time, view)` and `plan.risePose(time, view)` give either side at any time of the segment. `plan.dive(time)` and `plan.rise(time)` pick the side with `sideAt`.
- `handoverOf(segment)` gives `{kind, time, firstFrame, lastFrame, placeFirstFrame, placeLastFrame, frames}` or null. `coverWindow(segment)` is null for a stop that opted in; `judgedWindow(segment)` is what tools should use.
- T sits on a shutter middle: `centre = floor((start + at * length) * fps + 1e-9)`, `first = centre - floor((n - 1) / 2)`, `last = first + n - 1`, `T = ((first + last) / 2 + shutterFraction / 2) / fps`. Mars with the defaults: dive frame 2454 (T = 2454.25 / 30), place window 2442 to 2454; rise frame 2700 (T = 2700.25 / 30), place window 2700 to 2712.
- Inside a dissolve frame the weight is constant: `smooth((frame - first + 1) / (n + 1))` in a dive, 1 minus that in a rise. A sample at shutter position s draws the close side when `w > fract(s * 8 + floor(s * 8) * 0.618)`. With `dissolveFrames` 1 the weight is 0.5: half the samples of that one frame come from each side, and the draft tier (one sample, s 0.5, threshold 0.472) draws it from the close side exactly at T.
- `placeWeight(segment, time)`: dive 0 at `placeFirstFrame / fps`, smooth to 1 at `firstFrame / fps`, then 1; rise 1 until `(lastFrame + 1) / fps`, smooth to 0 at `(placeLastFrame + 1) / fps`.
- Softness (`motion.softFromPx`) and the cover do not exist in the dive and rise of a stop that opted in. Everything else in the film is as before, byte for byte.
- Once a stop opts in, `cut/CUES.md` and `cut/SHOTS.md` are stale until `npm run cues` and `npm run shots` run (both files belong to /root).
