### 13:10 to 13:50, a fault of E2's pass at the horizon, and its cure (the limb hold)
- Seen on dive frames 2445 to 2453 and growing through the rise from 2690: a dark line along the horizon, between the ground and the haze of the picture (8 pixels high at 2447).
- Why. The camera of the place window is above the twin, so its limb lies beyond the horizon of the picture. A sky ray between the two was below the twin's horizon, so the pass called it unseen (weight 0) and the globe's own sky showed: dark, with stars, where the close view has its bright dust haze. The ground between the twin's horizon and the limb (one to a few pixels, foreshortened) had no weight either.
- The picture's horizon itself (dumps `out/work/e4/matte/f2454-dump-image.png`, `-alpha.png`): haze meets haze near window row 540, no relief stands above the horizon row, the far mesas start 20 to 55 texels below it. So the row of the horizon may be reused freely.
- The cure, in `src/core/place.ts` (LAY and its mirror `placeUv`), three parts, all off when the reach is 0:
  1. Sky lift. `gap` = the camera's dip minus the twin's dip in the direction of the pixel (`uLimb`: camera up minus twin up, and the difference of the two sines). A sky pixel `own` above the limb looks the picture up `gap * (1 - smoothstep(0, 1, own / max(reach, 2 |gap|)))` higher along the twin's up: at the limb it reads the picture's horizon, at the reach it reads where the twin saw it. Symmetric: a camera below the twin squeezes. The weight of a sky pixel is then whole (`honest` 1).
  2. Ground clamp and hold band. A hit point beyond the twin's horizon looks up the horizon row of the picture at its own azimuth. `hold = 1 - smoothstep(limbHoldDeg[0], limbHoldDeg[1], angle under the limb)` and the weight is at least place weight times the edges times `hold`, so the band under the limb keeps the picture where `seen` and `maxEnlarge` would drop it.
  3. Derivatives. A lifted sky pixel and a clamped ground pixel take the size of a pixel in the picture from the algebra of a direction (`placeSkyGrad` is the mirror), not from `dFdx` and `dFdy`. Why: on the limb a 2 by 2 block holds sky and ground, the picture coordinate jumps inside it (16 texels at 2447), the smaller singular value of that false Jacobian falls under the `maxEnlarge` limit and the pixel loses its weight. Seen as single dark pixels at the stair corners of the limb in the dive (2447: 1421,310 and 1787,314) and as a dashed dark line in the rise (2696, x 1485 to 1900).
- Numbers: `config.handover.place.limbReachDeg` 0 (off film wide) and `limbHoldDeg` [0.25, 0.75]; a stop turns it on per kind with `handover: { dive: { place: { limbReachDeg: 8 } } }` and may give its own `limbHoldDeg`. Mars has 8 on both kinds, from `src/stops/mars/place.json` with a `limbNote`. Harness option `limb=DEG` on the diag string overrides the stop.
- Why off by default: a body without air has stars down to its horizon in both views, so there is nothing dark in the gap, and the lift would move the picture's stars against the globe's own (double stars near the limb). It is for a close view whose horizon carries a haze the globe side does not draw.

### 16:50 to 17:10, the tone split (`toneIn`)
- Idea: the picture is split in two. Its detail comes in as before (weight by time, edgeIn). - While the picture is drawn: `gradUniform` times scale and `setFine(scale)` (so `uFine` = scale and `uBias` = log2(scale) in the relief shader: mask and alpha lookups take the same mip level as in the frame, apron alpha gradients are per frame pixel), `view.place` true, `view.placeScale` the scale, `view.format` the frame format times scale. A set whose shader uses screen derivatives or implicit mip levels must scale them by `fineUniform` and `biasUniform` from `src/diagnostics/diag.ts` (both 1 and 0 outside the place picture).
- The pass (`Place.render`, after world, near and belt, before `over` and the cover): per pixel a ray against the unit sphere of the stop in camera relative coordinates; a hit projects the sphere point into the twin, a miss projects its direction as a point at infinity. Out = under * (1 - k) + k * (sky * (1 - A) + RGB * lens undo at this pixel). The sky is a copy of the sample target taken right after the sky and dust are drawn (`keepSky`); in the place window the world is drawn without the early pass.
- k = place weight * front * edge feather (inside the margin: `place.edge` of it) * `smoothstep(0, place.seen, honest)` * the enlargement limit (`place.maxEnlarge`, `place.enlargeSoft`). honest for a hit = (n . (twin - P)) / (n . (camera - P)) * height ratio: 0 at the twin's horizon, 1 at T everywhere; for a miss = (sin elevation seen from the twin + sin dip of the twin) / (the same for the camera).
- Sampling: `textureGrad` with the screen derivatives of the picture coordinate times `grad / scale`, capped at `place.maxGradTexels`. At T that is one texel at a texel centre: the close side's own pixel.
- The twin: `StopPlan.twin(local, time)`, a similarity from the close scene onto the unit sphere. Up of the close scene in terrain frame coordinates a = (0, cos tilt, sin tilt), tilt = horizonDeg + dipDeg, rolled by rollDeg; ground sphere radius rho = h0 / (1 / cos(dip) - 1), h0 = `terrain.ground ?? motion.terrainGround`. `twinFrame(time)` = {rotation: turn(time) * terrainFrame * R^-1 (R takes y to a), origin: the turned spot * (1 + h0 / rho), scale: 1 / rho}. A plan may override `twin` (Earth, Neptune do); the place, the pass, the globe camera at T and the sky carry all go through `plan.twin` only.
- The globe side of a stop that opted in (moves.ts): `descent(time)` and `ascent(time)` = `glide(far = hold pose or the pose after the rise, low = twin(close pose(time)), share)`, share = `trapeze` of the time between the far end and T (`descent.rise`, `descent.fall`). The glide keeps the look target (the point the twin looks at on the ground, at most `descent.targetReach` of the way to its horizon) and mixes log height, direction from the target and the rotation against the look frame. After T (dive) and before T (rise) the globe pose is the twin of the close pose, so the camera is C1 at T. E3 replaces this curve by a rate limited one.
- The sky through T (film.ts, globe side only): `Shot.skyShare` = `skyWeight` (0 until `skyFrames` before T, 1 at T); the glow is mixed to `terrainSkyGlow` by it, the skyLow factor fades out by it, both sky patches are passed with weights (1 - share) and share; the sky turn slerps `sky.orbits` to `sky.turns` by `skyTurnWeight` (0 at the far end of the segment, 1 at T, smootherstep); the sky quaternion is `slerp(1, E, turnWeight) * camera`, E = Qclose_world(tc) * Qtwin_world(tc)^-1 with tc the time clamped to the place window. At T the sky quaternion, the turn, the exposure and the glow are equal on both sides (tested).
- Near plane of the globe side of a stop that opted in: `min(0.0004, 0.2 * height)` as the floor, so the twin at 0.001 radii does not clip the ground.

Absent or all 0: off, the pass and its shader are the ones they were. No stop sets it (Mail 342: it stays off). It cures a picture that stands on the globe as a lighter or darker slab; it does nothing for a side that shows as a line of detail (that is edgeIn) or for ground that is not the same ground (that is the plates).
