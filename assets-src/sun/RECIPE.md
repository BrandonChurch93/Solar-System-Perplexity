### public/assets/sun/<set>/wrap-color.jpg

- Output: 8192 x 4096 and 4096 x 2048, JPEG quality 94, 4:4:4.
- Tool: `cd assets-src/sun/orbit && python3 wrap.py ../../../public/assets/sun/full/wrap-color.jpg ../../../public/assets/sun/web/wrap-color.jpg` (numpy, OpenCV; no model, no prompt).
- Steps: the 2x hero is unprojected onto the fitted sphere. On the side the hero shows, each pixel is decoded to scene values and divided by the measured limb curve (0.33 + 0.36 (1 - mu)) / 0.33, so the map holds the surface without the limb brightening and the shader puts the curve back at the real view angle. The hero is used where the view cosine is above 0.42 and fades out down to 0.2. The rest of the globe is filled from 12 copies of the hero's central cap (view cosine above 0.6 to 0.8), turned to the vertices of an icosahedron with a fixed random twist each (seed 7) and blended with wobbling weights raised to the 8th power, so the seams follow no straight line.
- Limb level (`level` and `level_at` in `wrap.py`): the fill comes from the centre of the hero, so at the limb it was too bright and too contrasty (white smears where the shader puts the limb curve back). On a grid of position angle and angle from the view axis (0.1 degree steps, 50 to 125 degrees from the axis) the hero and the fill are low-passed in log scene values (Gaussian, 1.3 degrees; the hero is trusted down to a view cosine of 0.12 and held beyond). The fill gets the hero's low-pass colour (gain limited to 0.12 to 3) and the hero's local contrast (ratio of the low-passed deviations, 0.35 to 1.15, median 0.53); highlights are capped at 1.0 above the low-pass in log, and green and blue may not rise more than 0.2 and 0.35 above red, so no highlight turns white. All of it fades out between 100 and 122 degrees from the axis.
- Take kept: the third. The first had no limb level (white smears at 0.93 to 0.99 radii), the second had the level without the highlight cap (one grey fleck left at the upper left limb).
- Phase 6 polish, item 5b, finer cells toward the limb (`assets-src/sun/orbit/cells.py`, called by `wrap.py` before the files are written; no model, no prompt, seeded with `np.random.default_rng(11)`). The layer is the hero's own fine structure at two thirds of its size: the hero's centre cap in log scene values with the limb curve divided out, minus its 12 px Gaussian, clipped to plus or minus 0.7. Spots and plage (the lowest 12 and the highest 10 percent of the 20 px low pass, the roughest 10 percent, grown by 22 px) are masked out and filled from ten shifted copies of the plain granulation. It is put on the sphere through 40 caps (Fibonacci centres, a fixed random twist each, zoom 1.5, the wobbling weights of the fill), so it is uniform on the sphere and foreshortens toward the limb by itself. `apply` adds it in log scene values, `scene * exp(e * layer - mean)`: e is 0.8 times a weight by the hero's view cosine (0 above 0.62, full below 0.4 and on the far side) times a dark mask (0 where the 10 px low pass of the map's log luminance lies 1.3 under the map's median, full from 0.8 under it, so umbra and penumbra keep their own strands). The bright side of the layer is capped at 0.3 so the dark lanes win, and `mean` (log of the mean of exp over the layer, by e) keeps the scene mean. The approved pixels stay the low frequencies; where the hero's view cosine is above 0.62 the map is the same as before. A third argument to `wrap.py` (a layer saved with `python3 cells.py FILE`) only saves time in trials.
- Take kept for 5b: the second (gain 0.8, cap 0.3). The first (gain 0.5, cap 0.25) left the limb ring short of the hero's fine contrast. Rms of the luminance minus its 1.5 px blur at frame 360 (draft tier, 1920 px), by quadrant, at 0.88 to 0.94 radii: hero 12.8 to 14.6, before 11.5 to 13.8, first take 11.9 to 14.2, second take 12.7 to 14.7. At 0.94 to 0.985 radii: hero 11.6 to 12.6, before 7.9 to 8.7, first 8.5 to 9.3, second 9.4 to 10.3. Inside 0.8 radii nothing changes (13.0 against 13.0).
- Checks: hold compare, the film disc lands on the hero disc with the same texture and colour; native crops of the left and right limb at frames 270, 360 and 449 beside the hero.

### public/assets/sun/<set>/corona.webp

- Output: 8192 x 1024 and 4096 x 512, lossless WebP. Columns are the angle around the disc centre (counterclockwise from image right), rows are the distance from the centre on a log scale from 1 to 3 radii, bottom row at the limb.
- Tool: `cd assets-src/sun/orbit && python3 halo.py && python3 card.py ../../../public/assets/sun corona` (numpy, scipy, OpenCV; no model, no prompt). The level pass below runs after it.
- Steps in `halo.py`: the 2x hero is unwrapped to polar form (`polar.py`). The prominences are matted out (see the next entry) and the corona behind them is estimated from a morphological closing of the blue channel times the corona's colour ratio per row. Stars are taken out above 1.07 radii with a 7 x 7 opening. The sky that is subtracted is the low-pass of the film's own sky card (`infinity.py`, see `sky-hold.webp`), so corona plus card gives the hero's level back. Hero data is used as far out as the sky is under 0.3 of the total light (`TRUST`, never closer than 1.3 radii), or to 0.03 radii inside the frame edge where the frame ends sooner; the reach is smoothed around the disc (median 1.43 radii, 1.3 where the Milky Way lies behind the corona). Beyond that each ray continues as a power law (exponent measured between 1.12 and 1.26 radii, 8.0, clipped to 4 to 8) from its own median level. From 1.25 to 1.42 radii outward every ray is smoothed along the radius (0.03 in ln r) and made monotone, so no star remnant or Milky Way cloud stays in it. Everything fades out between 2.4 and 3 radii.
- Level pass: `assets-src/sun/orbit/match.py`. With `assets-src/sun/orbit/level.npy` and `assets-src/sun/orbit/veil.json` deleted, build corona and card, `npm run build`, `npm run still -- --frame 360 --tier review --out out/work/sun/h360-raw.png`, then `cd assets-src/sun/orbit && python3 match.py ../../../out/work/sun/h360-raw.png && python3 card.py ../../../public/assets/sun corona && python3 sky.py ../../../public/assets/sun`. `match.py` compares the star-free floor of that frame with the hero's in polar form, in scene values, and writes a smooth factor for the corona (`level.npy`, 512 x 128, 6 degrees across and 0.02 in ln r, limits 0.3 to 1.5) and the part the corona cannot absorb as a function of radius (`veil.json`), which `sky.py` takes out of the card. It accounts for the veil of the lens bloom and for the sky that the corona hides (`hide` in `src/stops/sun/look.ts`). One pass, not repeated.
- Take kept: the sixth version of the split. Earlier versions left streaks (row interpolation), dark strands (blue channel alone), or kept ray ends in the sky card.
- Round 10 rebuild (no new generation takes): `python3 stars.py` runs first. It finds the hero's stars (what a 13 pixel line opening in 8 directions cannot keep), drops anything longer than 11 pixels within 1.7 radii as a strand of the Sun, and writes `out/work/sun/hero-free.npy`, `hero-stars.npy` and `hero-starmask.npy`. `halo.py` works on the star free hero, adds the fine detail back to the rays, and lifts the trusted reach where it finds a thin orange strand outside the prominence matte (brighter than a 31 x 31 opening by 0.35 of it plus 0.004, area over 40), so the far end of a strand stays on the Sun's layer. `look.corona.hide` is 0.3 to 0.9 of the corona's light.
- Phase 6 polish, item 6 (no generation takes, seeded with `np.random.default_rng(23)`): `streamers` in `halo.py` (`STREAM`). Beyond the approved frame's edge of each column plus 0.006 radii the continuation is no longer one power law for every ray. The light is split into a broad part (a Gaussian of 60 columns round the disc) and the fine rays. The broad part gets a power that changes with the angle (the measured 8.0 plus or minus 4.5, 3 to 12 waves round the disc, never under 3). The fine rays fade over a length that changes with the angle (0.3 in ln r times e to the power of plus or minus 1.5, in groups of 6 to 24 and rays of 40 to 160 waves round the disc). A lean of at most 0.12 rad per unit of ln r bends them. Every factor is 1 at the edge. Inside the frame edge the polar corona is equal bit for bit and the card changes only above 1.189 radii (largest change 15 code values), so the level pass was not run again: `level.npy` and `veil.json` are as they were, and frame 360 at the draft tier keeps its bytes. `halo.py` takes about 4 GB of memory; do not run it beside a render.
- Checks: hold compare; frame 360 with the sky card switched off shows a whole corona with no wedge or edge; floors of film and hero by zone (far 0.032 against 0.032, 1.5 to 1.9 radii 0.061 against 0.059, 1.25 to 1.5 radii 0.167 against 0.166).

### Shaders and values

- The ground streams (`life.ts` `alive()`, values `life.json` `flow`): the picture is looked up twice along the stream of the map, half a period apart (`flow.period` 0.5 s). Each look starts half its travel upstream and ends half its travel downstream (`flow.travel` 0.36 of a cell on the flank of a cell) and counts for nothing at both ends, so nothing drifts and nothing jumps. The picture is taken apart into its broad shapes (the lookup through a footprint `flow.blur` 8 times as wide) and the fine texture on them: the fine texture runs the whole way, the broad shapes `flow.slow` 0.3 of it, so a cell keeps its outline and its lanes. Where the two looks are far apart their mean is brought back toward the strength of one (`flow.keep`). What has just come up is brighter, what nears the lane darker (`flow.age`, in stops). Every place has its own shift of the clock (`flow.offset`, slow noise of the place, `apart`), except near the moments `flow.still` (seconds of the ground's time, which is nil at frame 90: frames 0, 60 and 90), where the shifts close to nil over `flow.ease` seconds and every look stands at its own place: there the frame is the picture itself. The plate and the apron run by the same length (`reach`).
- On top of the stream, as light in stops: fronts from the deepest place of a cell to its lanes (`wave`), knots of light made by noise whose third axis is the depth and the time (`made`, `shine`), the slow dome of a cell and its rim (`dome`), cells that close and open (`close`), the push of a cell on its neighbours (`swell`, at most 0.06 of a cell), grains in the lanes (`grain`), soft grains along the fibres of the penumbra (`stream`), dots in the dark core (`dots`).
- Cells that split (`life.json` `split`): at each of three sites (`sites`, the place of the new lane's middle on the ground in cell sizes, its turn and half length, from the list of `cells.py`) a dark point opens at its own time (`at`, seconds of the ground's time, all after frame 90) and grows over `takes` 1.33 s (40 frames) into a lane of `width` cell widths that wanders a little (`wander`); the stream on both sides turns toward the new lane (`pull`, strongest `range` cell widths away), the fronts reach it last, the knots keep out of it, its flanks are shaded (`shade`) and it takes the colour of a lane (`tint`). It is a function of the place on the ground, so the plate, the apron and the globe side draw the same lane.
- A thin haze low over the ground, close side only, zero where the view is steeper than `haze.steep` (so zero at T): `ground.ts`, values `life.json` `haze`.
- `src/stops/sun/life.ts` (`LIFE_GLSL`), included by the close ground (`ground.ts`) and by the globe (`globe.ts`), which share its uniforms by name (`LIFE_NAMES`), so both sides are in the same state at every time. Values: `src/stops/sun/life.json`.
- `src/stops/sun/loops.ts` holds the life of the arcade; values: `src/stops/sun/arcade.json`:
  - `sway`: the strands sway (as before).
  - `flow`: the light of a strand is carried by clumps that run from foot to foot. Two layers of lanes (`layers`: lanes per unit of top height, spacing, length, seed; lengths in distances between the feet), each a factor on the light: `low` between the clumps, more in a clump, mean over time one. `speed` per layer in distances between the feet per second, `back` the share of lanes that runs the other way, `share` the share of clumps that are there, `head` how soft the head is, `knee` how the gain bends on bright strands, `ends` where the flow fades at the feet, `inside` the angle from which the outermost strands take no flow. `lift` raises what a clump adds (the knee takes about a tenth off the bright parts). Since mail 296 `low` is 0.72 and 0.82 (was 0.35 and 0.55): between two clumps a strand keeps 0.59 of its light (was 0.19), so a clump is a change of brightness along a thread and the thread never falls to nothing.
  - `lead`: a few strands lead and the many are faint. Two sets of lanes across the strands (`lanes` per unit of top height, about 18 px at the top of the loop at frame 90), half a lane apart and each seen through a window, so no seam shows; a `share` of the lanes leads. A leading strand takes `bright` times its light and a soft body (the picture seen wide, times `body`, which runs with the clumps and is held under `most`); the others take `faint`.
  - `rain`: knots that fall down both legs and speed up, in gusts.
  - `feet`: the feet flicker and light the ground.
  - What rain, feet and lights add to a strand is one sum that is bent over where the strand is bright already (`lights.knee`: the sum is divided by 1 + knee times the sum times the strand's own light), so they cannot burn a strand out; the soft body of a leading strand takes `lights.body` of a lit loop.
  - `core.most`: no sample of the arcade is brighter than this (scene value, 3 is display 250), or than the picture itself where the picture has more. The bound is applied to each sample before the samples of a pixel are added up (mail 260 point 5), with the hue kept.
  - `lights`: the loops of the arcade light up one after another (time in s since the start of the terrain segment, angle of the loop, width, amount); each lights from its feet to its top in `climb`, stays `keep`, cools with `fall`. Since mail 296: `keep` 0.3 and `fall` 0.45 s (were 0.5 and 1.2), so about two loops are lit at a time and one is always arriving while another leaves; the first lights at 2.25 s, after the judged frame 60.
  - `thrown`: the arc that the release throws beyond the loop top stays, opens at its top from `open`, thins and frays, and is down at `down`. No strand ends in a cut (mail 260 point 2): below the opening a strand breaks up over a stretch (`soft` plus `break` times how far the opening has come) into gaps, then clumps, then single drops (`drops` per unit of the way along), which run down the leg with the opening (`drift`) and are dim next to it (`dim`); `ends` is the light of the falling ends and how far it reaches.
  - The timing of the event itself (foot, surge, flash, throw) is `look.ts` `opening`.   - What the release throws on a foot and on the ground under it lifts them toward `opening.surge.most` (0.45 in scene values, display 200, the line I count as near white) and adds nothing where they are brighter already.
