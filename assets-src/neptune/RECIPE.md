# Neptune assets

Every file exists in `public/assets/neptune/full/` and `public/assets/neptune/web/`. The prompts of every generated input are kept word for word as the tool calls in `assets-src/neptune/prompts/`. Rejected takes and intermediate files stay under `out/work/nep/`.

## public/assets/neptune/<set>/wrap.webp

- What: the globe map, 8192x4096 RGBA for `full`, 4096x2048 for `web`. RGB = cloud free deck albedo (sRGB of albedo times 0.5), A = cirrus opacity. Lossless alpha (`exact`), quality 92.
- Tools: `python3 tools/ml/upscale.py` (2x hero), then `assets-src/neptune/wrap/unproject.py`, `curves.py`, `sepmap.py`, `assemble.py`, in that order. `sepmap.py` also writes the cirrus light curve into `src/stops/neptune/curve.json`. After any change of `limb.veil*` in `src/stops/neptune/haze.json` run `sepmap.py` and `assemble.py` again.
- Inputs: `references/approved/neptune-hero.png`, `src/stops/neptune/fit.json`, `src/stops/neptune/haze.json`, and the generated tiles under `out/work/nep/gen/`.
- Hero side: the hero's own pixels, 2x, unprojected to the band aligned map, divided by the measured light curve and the limb veil, split into deck and cirrus by least squares against the cloud colour (`CLOUD_TINT` in `sepmap.py`: green over blue 0.43 at ndl 0.3, 0.48 at 0.6, 0.50 at 1; red 0.30) times the cloud light curve `CLOUD_VALUES`. That curve is the envelope of the hero's brightest cirrus per ndl bin (raised 1.1 to 1.25 times at 09:27, since the top 0.3 percent of the hero's cirrus stood above the first curve), so opacity 1 reaches the hero's cores. Round 2: the five knots round the terminator are lifted (`LIFT` in `sepmap.py`: ndl 0 to `[0.24, 0.245, 0.235]`, 0.0625 to `[0.2, 0.235, 0.28]`, 0.125 to `[0.315, 0.39, 0.56]`), because the hero's brightest tufts just past the terminator are near white at 0.24 where the curve gave 0.06, so they came out as flat grey shapes of opacity 1; the cloud mask still uses the curve without the lift. Where the long range deck fill under the mask finds no lit deck (the twilight strip), the deck is now the local 40th percentile of the hero's albedo instead of black, which removes the dark blotches round the twilight tufts. Hero pixels fade out below mu 0.42 and below ndl 0.2; the cirrus alpha is kept down to ndl -0.06 so the twilight clouds are the hero's. The cirrus opacity is the least squares fit of the hero's rise above the deck toward the cloud colour over red and green only (`FIT = [0.5, 0.5, 0]`): the hero's blue is at 255 wherever the cirrus is bright, and a fit that includes it turns every bright core into a hard fleck of opacity 1.
- Far side: generated tiles, image tool model `seedream_5`, one take each. Reference inputs: `out/work/nep/wrap/ref-deck.jpg` (a crop of the separated hero deck in map space) and `out/work/nep/wrap/ref-cirrus.jpg` (a crop of the separated hero cirrus matte).
  - Deck: `deck-m0`, `deck-m2` (prompts in `prompts/tiles-1.js`), `deck-m1b` (`prompts/tiles-2.js`; it replaces `deck-m1`, whose bands bowed), polar caps `deck-n`, `deck-s` on a polar projection.
  - Cirrus: `cir-m0` to `cir-m2`, `cir-n`, `cir-s` (`prompts/tiles-1.js`), `cir-m3` to `cir-m5` (`prompts/tiles-2.js`).
  - The opacity of the generated cirrus tiles is scaled (`CIRRUS`, `CIRRUS_CAPS` in `assemble.py`: 0.8 for the wide tiles, 0.65 for the equator, 0.56 for the caps) so the far side is no brighter than the hero's side under the same curve. Round 2: the generated opacity is also multiplied, per map texel, by the ratio of the cloud curve without the lift to the curve with it at that texel's ndl (0.27 at ndl 0, 0.60 at 0.0625, 0.72 at 0.125, 0.88 at -0.0625, 1 elsewhere), so the lift that the hero's twilight tufts need does not brighten the generated cirrus along the terminator as much.
  - Only the detail of a deck tile is used (log ratio to an anisotropic low pass, clamped). Its colour comes from the hero's zonal median profile, so tiles cannot differ in colour and there is no seam. Cirrus tiles cross-fade by maximum.
- Takes: 1 of 6 for every tile, 2 for the middle deck tile.
- Checks: recomposite error against the hero in the well lit part, mean 1.20 of 255, p99 16 (2.50 and 36 with the blue channel in the fit, 1.49 and 18 before the cloud curve was raised); disc medians of the judged hold within 3 percent of the hero for ndl 0.1 to 0.8 (`check/ratio.py`). Still to check from the rise angle: the seam at lon 180, the poles, the lighter lens of the hero inset.

## public/assets/neptune/<set>/hold.webp

- What: the cirrus of the approved hero for the judged hold, as a second layer over the hero's window of the globe map: 4552x3641 RGBA for `full` (3.36 MB), 2276x1821 for `web` (0.79 MB). RGB = the same deck colour as `wrap.webp` in that window, A = cirrus opacity divided by 1.5, so opacities up to 1.5 are kept (the wrap clips them at 1). Lossless alpha (`exact`), quality 92 (`web` 88). Its place in the map and the head room are written to `src/stops/neptune/hold.json` (`box`, `head`). No new generation: it is made from the hero's own pixels.
- Tools: `python3 assets-src/neptune/wrap/sepmap.py`, then `python3 assets-src/neptune/wrap/assemble.py` (function `hold()`), after the steps of `wrap.webp`. `sepmap.py`: `HEAD = 1.5`, `HOLD_MU = (0.22, 0.27)`; it keeps the unclipped opacity (`over`) and the weights `hweight`, `haweight` in `out/work/nep/wrap/hero-layers.npz`.
- Inputs: the same as `wrap.webp` (`references/approved/neptune-hero.png`, `src/stops/neptune/fit.json`, `src/stops/neptune/haze.json`).
- Why: in the wrap the hero's cirrus is clipped at opacity 1 and faded out toward the limb from mu 0.42 to 0.22, which took 6 to 15 levels off the brightest crests of the judged hold (storm rim, the bands toward the right limb). The layer keeps the hero's cirrus up to opacity 1.5 and unfaded down to mu 0.27 to 0.22. It is shown only near the hold's view (`uHold.x = home * amount` in `set.ts`; 0 in the terrain, the rise and the ending), so the far views keep the wrap.
- Takes: 2 of 6. Take 1 also took the hero's deck unfaded down to mu 0.22 to 0.27; its dark trough with a hard edge under the limb veil moved the judged hold away from the hero (mean abs diff 4.5484 to 4.6758) and was dropped. Take 2, kept: the deck weight is the wrap's (fade 0.22 to 0.42), only the cirrus opacity is the hero's.
- Checks: `wrap.webp` (both sets) and `curve.json` byte identical after the rebuild (md5); recomposite with opacity up to 1.5 against the hero: mean 1.21 of 255, p99 16, share of texels over opacity 1: 0.0013; judged hold 4605 crest tops and mean abs diff in `NOTES.md` (Phase 6, item 4).
