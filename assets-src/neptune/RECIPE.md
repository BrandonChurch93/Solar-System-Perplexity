# Neptune assets

Every file exists in `public/assets/neptune/full/` and `public/assets/neptune/web/`. The prompts of every generated input are kept word for word as the tool calls in `assets-src/neptune/prompts/`. Rejected takes and intermediate files stay under `out/work/nep/`.

## public/assets/neptune/<set>/wrap.webp

- What: the globe map, 8192x4096 RGBA for `full`, 4096x2048 for `web`. RGB = cloud free deck albedo (sRGB of albedo times 0.5), A = cirrus opacity. Lossless alpha (`exact`), quality 92.
- Tools: `python3 tools/ml/upscale.py` (2x hero), then `assets-src/neptune/wrap/unproject.py`, `curves.py`, `sepmap.py`, `assemble.py`, in that order. `sepmap.py` also writes the cirrus light curve into `src/stops/neptune/curve.json`. After any change of `limb.veil*` in `src/stops/neptune/haze.json` run `sepmap.py` and `assemble.py` again.
- Inputs: `references/approved/neptune-hero.png`, `src/stops/neptune/fit.json`, `src/stops/neptune/haze.json`, and the generated tiles under `out/work/nep/gen/`.
- Hero side: the hero's own pixels, 2x, unprojected to the band aligned map, divided by the measured light curve and the limb veil, split into deck and cirrus by least squares against the cloud colour (`CLOUD_TINT` in `sepmap.py`: green over blue 0.43 at ndl 0.3, 0.48 at 0.6, 0.50 at 1; red 0.30) times the cloud light curve `CLOUD_VALUES`. That curve is the envelope of the hero's brightest cirrus per ndl bin (raised 1.1 to 1.25 times at 09:27, since the top 0.3 percent of the hero's cirrus stood above the first curve), so opacity 1 reaches the hero's cores. Hero pixels fade out below mu 0.42 and below ndl 0.2; the cirrus alpha is kept down to ndl -0.06 so the twilight clouds are the hero's. The cirrus opacity is the least squares fit of the hero's rise above the deck toward the cloud colour over red and green only (`FIT = [0.5, 0.5, 0]`): the hero's blue is at 255 wherever the cirrus is bright, and a fit that includes it turns every bright core into a hard fleck of opacity 1.
- Far side: generated tiles, image tool model `seedream_5`, one take each. Reference inputs: `out/work/nep/wrap/ref-deck.jpg` (a crop of the separated hero deck in map space) and `out/work/nep/wrap/ref-cirrus.jpg` (a crop of the separated hero cirrus matte).
  - Deck: `deck-m0`, `deck-m2` (prompts in `prompts/tiles-1.js`), `deck-m1b` (`prompts/tiles-2.js`; it replaces `deck-m1`, whose bands bowed), polar caps `deck-n`, `deck-s` on a polar projection.
  - Cirrus: `cir-m0` to `cir-m2`, `cir-n`, `cir-s` (`prompts/tiles-1.js`), `cir-m3` to `cir-m5` (`prompts/tiles-2.js`).
  - The opacity of the generated cirrus tiles is scaled (`CIRRUS`, `CIRRUS_CAPS` in `assemble.py`: 0.8 for the wide tiles, 0.65 for the equator, 0.56 for the caps) so the far side is no brighter than the hero's side under the same curve.
  - Only the detail of a deck tile is used (log ratio to an anisotropic low pass, clamped). Its colour comes from the hero's zonal median profile, so tiles cannot differ in colour and there is no seam. Cirrus tiles cross-fade by maximum.
- Takes: 1 of 6 for every tile, 2 for the middle deck tile.
- Checks: recomposite error against the hero in the well lit part, mean 1.20 of 255, p99 16 (2.50 and 36 with the blue channel in the fit, 1.49 and 18 before the cloud curve was raised); disc medians of the judged hold within 3 percent of the hero for ndl 0.1 to 0.8 (`check/ratio.py`). Still to check from the rise angle: the seam at lon 180, the poles, the lighter lens of the hero inset.

## public/assets/neptune/<set>/sky-hold.webp

- What: the sky patch of the hold, 3520x1980 lossless (`web` 1760x990): the hero's own sky with margin 0.125.
- Tool: `python3 assets-src/neptune/sky/patch.py hold references/approved/neptune-hero.png out/work/nep/sky/fill-t1.png out/work/nep/sky/glow-only.png public/assets/neptune`.
- Inputs: the hero; `out/work/nep/sky/glow-only.png`, a review still of frame 4605 rendered with `sky.json` gain 0 (the film's own glow, ring off); the generated fill `out/work/nep/sky/fill-t1.png`.
- Fill: image tool model `gpt_image_2_6_flare`, input the hero, prompt in `prompts/sky-fill.js`. Take 1 of 6, kept: it continues the band behind the globe and keeps the hero's stars in place outside it.
- What the script does: sky = hero minus the film's glow. The hero's own sky is used from 1.07 radii out, except under Triton, the ring and the film's glow. Elsewhere the fill, with its low frequencies matched to the hero. Outside the disc the hero's own stars replace the fill's. Round 2: the fill's stars are kept only behind the disc (`BEHIND (0.95, 0.99)` radii), so none shows through the haze band at the limb (they read as a row of dots along the dark upper left limb). The margin is not a mirror: the star free sky at the hero's edge is carried outward and blurred more with distance (6, 18 and 48 pixels), with the Sun removed, so no star or cloud of the hero appears twice.
- Checks: the radial blue medians round the Sun equal the hero's at the review tier; no rim where the patch meets the one sky at the fixed sky aim.

## public/assets/neptune/<set>/ring-bands.png

- What: the ring's fine structure in ring coordinates (radius 1.28 to 1.92 by azimuth), 4096x512 (`web` 2048x256), designed, not generated and not measured. Red: 16 ringlets and a grainy sheet (square root coded, 1 = 0.16). Green: the bright arcs and sparse glints (square root coded, 1 = 1.0). Blue (round 2): five broad band bodies with fine radial grain and slow variation round the ring (`BODIES`, square root coded, 1 = 0.05), used only away from the hold (`look.ring.bandHold[2] = 0`, `bandAway[2] = 1.6`) so the ring reads as bands of material in the rise and the ending.
- Tool: `python3 assets-src/neptune/ring/bands.py` (seed 20261121, no image tool, no take).
- Why designed: the hero's dark side is heavily block coded, so its crisp ringlets cannot be measured; `ring.png` keeps only the broad glow. The ringlet radii follow the hero's strand peaks (1.51 to 1.53, 1.60 to 1.62, 1.65 to 1.69, 1.77, 1.377) and the two bright arcs sit at the hero's clumps (radius 1.641 at ring azimuth -117.0 degrees, radius 1.512 at -121.3), with five fainter ones.
- Use: `ring.ts` adds it to `ring.png` in the hold (`look.ring.bandHold`) and draws it alone away from the hold (`look.ring.bandAway`), where the three analytic strands and the flat sheet used to be.
- Checks: boosted crop of the lower left dark side at frame 4605 against the hero (`out/work/nep/ring-ll-boost2.jpg`); frame 5006 shows many bands of differing brightness instead of three outlines. Read with `textureGrad` and mipmaps, anisotropy 16.

## public/assets/neptune/<set>/close-deck.webp, close-cloud.webp, close-deck-wide.webp, close-cloud-wide.webp, close-height.png

The five maps are `public/assets/neptune/<set>/close-deck.webp`, `public/assets/neptune/<set>/close-cloud.webp`, `public/assets/neptune/<set>/close-deck-wide.webp`, `public/assets/neptune/<set>/close-cloud-wide.webp` and `public/assets/neptune/<set>/close-height.png`.

- What: the terrain view. `references/approved/neptune-close.jpg` is projected from its fitted camera onto a sphere and split into a blue deck, a raised cloud layer and a shadow factor, so the clouds have parallax against the deck, cast a moving shadow and stream along the band.
  - `close-deck.webp` 5632x3168 (`web` 2816x1584): the deck inside the approved frame, clouds removed, shadows divided out.
  - `close-cloud.webp` 5632x3168 RGBA: cloud colour and opacity inside the approved frame.
  - `close-deck-wide.webp`, `close-cloud-wide.webp` 4096x2304 (`web` 2048x1152): the same two layers on a canvas 1.75 times as wide and tall as the approved frame (same top edge), for everything the flight sees outside the approved frame.
  - `close-height.png` 2048x1152 (`web` 1024x576) on the wide canvas: R = cloud top height (0 to 1 of the slab), G = shadow factor on the deck.
- Tools, in order:
  1. `python3 assets-src/neptune/terrain/horizon.py 30` fits the horizon of the approved image and writes `src/stops/neptune/close.json` (pitch 20.63, roll -9.54, radius 41.81 camera heights, rms 0.029 degrees).
  2. `python3 tools/ml/upscale.py --in references/approved/neptune-close.jpg --out out/work/nep/terrain/close2x.png --width 5632`.
  3. `python3 assets-src/neptune/terrain/guide.py` writes `out/work/nep/terrain/guide.png`: the approved image in its place on the wide canvas, flat blue below the model horizon and black above it elsewhere.
  4. Image tool, prompts word for word in `prompts/canvas.js`, input `guide.png`: `canvas-t1` (model `gpt_image_2_6_flare`, 1672x941) and `canvas-t2` (model `seedream_5`). Take 1 of 6 kept: its centre stays on the approved image to 0.7 px. Take 2 rejected: it redrew and warped the centre.
  5. Image tool, prompt in `prompts/clean.js`, model `gpt_image_2_6_flare`: the clouds and their shadows removed. `clean-canvas-t1` (input `out/work/nep/terrain/canvas-base.png`, the approved image pasted into take 1) kept, take 1 of 6. `clean-centre-t1` (input the approved image) rejected: it enlarged the storm.
  6. `python3 tools/ml/upscale.py --in out/work/nep/terrain/canvas-t1.png --out out/work/nep/terrain/canvas-t1-2x.png --width 3344`.
  7. `python3 assets-src/neptune/terrain/layers.py` writes the five files.
- What `layers.py` does: the generated canvas is matched to the approved image (histogram match per channel inside the approved frame, then a smooth log gain taken at the frame border and carried outward; then `seam()`: the step in log tone and the ratio of local contrast between a 28 px strip inside and a 28 px strip outside the frame border are applied to the outside, falling off with an exponential reach of 900 px, because the generated canvas starts lighter and with greyer clouds exactly at the border; then `limb()`: in a strip from 24 px above to 320 px below the fitted horizon, read along the horizon (each column shifted by its own horizon row), the outside takes the inside's profile below the horizon: the log mean of the box within 50 px of the border, per distance below the horizon, replaces the outside's own running mean, full strength for 14 px into the box and falling off outward with a reach of 700 px, because the generated limb is a thin bright cyan line where the approved image has a soft blue band, and a camera above the fitted one sees that strip stretched), and the approved image is pasted in with a 14 px feather. The clean plate is scaled to the canvas where there is neither cloud nor shadow. Cloud opacity = how far the red to blue ratio rises from the clean plate toward white (`WHITE 0.9`, `MARGIN 0.07`). Shadow factor = smoothed luminance ratio to the clean plate near clouds with a dead zone of 0.1; before the smoothing `level()` lifts the ratio outside the frame border by its step in log across the border (cloud free pixels, 28 px strips, reach 900 px), so the factor has no step at the border: the shader reads it at the cloud's streamed position, and a step would slide off the border and show as a stripe. The same function knits two more things at the border at a fine scale: the clean plate, per channel, before alpha and the ratio are taken (3 px strips, reach 160 px), because the plate is the deck under the clouds and its step is uncovered when the clouds stream on; and the finished shadow factor (6 px strips, reach 90 px). Deck = image divided by the shadow factor outside clouds, the clean plate under clouds. Cloud colour is solved so that deck times shadow under cloud gives the image back. Height = blurred opacity times cloud brightness.
- Checks: recomposite error against the canvas, mean 0.20 of 255, p99 6; against the 2x approved image, mean 0.25, p99 6. Crops of `out/work/nep/terrain/wide.png` across the left and right frame borders show no step. The judged frame 4860 beside the approved image in `npm run compare -- --stop neptune --view terrain`.

## public/assets/neptune/<set>/close-glow.png and public/assets/neptune/<set>/sky-terrain.webp

- What: `close-glow.png` 512x64 is the blue glow above the horizon of the approved close image, unwrapped by azimuth about the nadir (80 degrees wide) and by height above the horizon (0 to 0.012 radii), stars removed. `sky-terrain.webp` 3520x1980 lossless (`web` 1760x990) is the sky of the approved close image without that glow, with margin 0.125.
- Tool: `python3 assets-src/neptune/sky/terrain.py` (no generation). Inputs: the approved close image and `src/stops/neptune/close.json`.
- What the script does: block grid removed, scene values, glow = smooth part above the far sky level, 45th percentile over 41 map columns along azimuth, then a 5 column blur, so no star gets into it (round 2: the 30th percentile over 141 columns left the glow at 0.76 to 0.83 of the approved image in the left and middle of the frame; now 0.99 to 1.08 in every fifth of the width). Sky = image minus glow; within 0.3 of the glow height the smooth part is taken from higher up so no line stays at the horizon. Above 0.34 of the glow height the sky is the image minus the glow as it is (round 2: taking the larger of the image and its 3 pixel smooth part there filled the dark between the spikes of the bright star, so the star came out round; the green row through it now follows the approved image and the sky above the glow is 0.83 levels from the approved image on average, was 1.54). Below the horizon the sky is not a mirror: it is the sky from 6 degrees higher, turned 14 degrees about the nadir, or the median sky level where that falls outside the image. The planet's turn moves the stars against the horizon by a few degrees during the view, so this part can come into sight. The margin is the star free sky carried outward as in the hold patch.
- Checks: frames 4770 and 4949 at the review tier show no line in the sky where the horizon was.

## public/assets/neptune/<set>/patch-96.webp, patch-64.webp, patch-40.webp, patch-26.webp, patch-16.webp, patch-10.webp

- Files: `public/assets/neptune/<set>/patch-96.webp`, `public/assets/neptune/<set>/patch-64.webp`, `public/assets/neptune/<set>/patch-40.webp`, `public/assets/neptune/<set>/patch-26.webp`, `public/assets/neptune/<set>/patch-16.webp`, `public/assets/neptune/<set>/patch-10.webp`.
- What: the dive patch levels, gnomonic squares round the dive spot (hero pixel 0.55, 0.52 = lon 0.23, lat -20.17), 96, 64, 40, 26, 16 and 10 degrees wide, 4096x4096 for `full`, 2048x2048 for `web`. RGBA like the wrap: RGB = deck albedo, A = cirrus opacity. Quality 92 (`web` 88), `exact`.
- Tools: `python3 assets-src/neptune/patches/bake.py` (uses `patches/gnomon.py` and `geo.py`), about 2 minutes. It also writes `src/stops/neptune/patches.json` (spot, level list, feather, fade thresholds).
- Inputs: `public/assets/neptune/full/wrap.webp` and two generated pictures.
  1. `python3 assets-src/neptune/patches/guide.py 24` writes `out/work/nep/patches/guide-24.png`, the wrap (deck with the cirrus over it, flat light) resampled to a 24 degree square at 2048.
  2. Image tool, prompt word for word in `prompts/patch-24.js`, input `guide-24.png`, 1:1. `patch24-t1` (model `seedream_5`, 2048x2048) kept, take 1 of 6: the layout stays (optical flow against the guide at 512 px: median 0.10 px, p99 0.56 px) and the cirrus becomes filaments, the storm gains spiral striations. `patch24-t2` (model `nano_banana_pro`) rejected: it returned the input unchanged.
  3. `bake.py` run once without the second picture writes `out/work/nep/patches/guide-12.png`: the inner 12 degrees of the wrap with take 1 laid in.
  4. Image tool, prompt in `prompts/patch-12.js`, input `guide-12.png`, model `seedream_5`: `patch12-t1` kept, take 1 of 6 (flow median 0.51 px at 512 px; the top edge lost a cloud streak, which lies inside the feather).
- What `bake.py` does with a take: gain in linear light so that its blur (1/48 of the width) equals the guide's; cloud opacity = how far the red channel rises above the wrap's deck toward white (`MARGIN 0.04`, `WHITE 0.93`), with its low frequencies corrected to the wrap's opacity; deck = picture with the cloud divided out, its red and green held to at most 1.3 times the wrap deck's ratio to blue, its low frequencies set to the wrap's deck, and the wrap's deck under cloud thicker than 0.3. The layers are laid into every level with an 8 percent feather at their edge. Where a level is finer than its sources, streak noise octaves are added (unit noise blurred 6 to 1 east to west, one octave per halving down to 0.8 texels, deck gain 0.04 in linear light, opacity 0.45 at cloud edges, seed 7).
- Checks: `out/work/nep/patches/separate-24.jpg` and `separate-12.jpg` (guide, matched take, recomposite, deck), `levels.jpg`. G13 on the dive and rise frames outside the cover, see NOTES.md.
