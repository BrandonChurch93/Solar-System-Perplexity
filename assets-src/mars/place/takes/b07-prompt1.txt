Task: repaint image 1 as one seamless, sharp nadir orbital photograph of this land, with the same framing, the same scale and north at the top.

1. The middle of image 1 (the square that takes three sevenths of its width, centred) is already final. Keep it as it is: every form, every shadow and its brightness. Carry its forms, its light and its shadows on outward, so that no border of that square can be seen.

2. Every form stays exactly where it is and keeps its size and outline: the canyon, its rims, every cliff, ledge, spur, mesa, gully and ridge. Nothing is moved, added or taken away.

3. Light and dark stay where they are. What is dark in image 1 stays just as dark, what is lit stays lit, at the same brightness. Do not brighten the shadows and do not darken the lit ground. The whole picture keeps the brightness of image 1.

4. The shadowed canyon floor and the shadowed feet of the walls are nearly black, with stepped walls, talus and boulders just to be made out in them, as the shadowed floor in image 2. The shadow is one body that follows the canyon: it has the shape of the canyon's floor and of the feet of its walls.

5. The outlines of the shadows become natural: ragged along the ground, following ledges and spurs, cast toward the lower right. Never straight, never a soft blob, never a flat even patch.

6. Seams and blurred, smeared or tiled parts get the same sharp natural rock as the rest, so that no border between parts can be seen.

7. Sharp natural detail everywhere, the same sharpness over the whole picture. No tiles, no blocks, no grid, no rows of equal boulders, no smear, no blur, no streaks, no etched lines, no bright outlines, no blue, no white. No cloud, no water. No new craters.

A photograph from orbit: no horizon, no sky, no text, no border, no vignette.
