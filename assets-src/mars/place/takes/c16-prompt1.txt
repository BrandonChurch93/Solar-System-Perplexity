The sketch is right about where the canyon runs and how wide it is, and wrong about what it looks like.

Task: redraw image 1 as one seamless nadir orbital photograph with the same framing, the same scale and north at the top.

1. The middle square, the right half and the high ground along the top and the bottom stay as they are, every light and dark patch where it is.

2. Left of the middle square draw the canyon on to the left edge as the same canyon that the middle shows, in the same place and with the same width as the sketch. Its north wall is sunlit: stepped cliffs of layered rock, the strata as thin bands of rust, tan and brown that follow the wall along its length, cut by spurs and gullies that run down from the rim to the floor, talus aprons at the foot. Its south wall is in shadow: the same cliffs, spurs and gullies in dark brown, the ground in them still to be made out. The floor is dim: rubble, low hummocks, a few landslide tongues. The rims are irregular, with alcoves and spurs, never a straight line. The canyon leaves the picture at the left edge at full width; it does not close, narrow or end.

3. No seam, no step and no change of sharpness or colour where the middle square meets the rest: one piece of land under one light.

4. No smear, no blur, no streaks, no flat cells, no bright outlines, no etched look. No pure black: the darkest shadow still shows ground. No mist, no cloud, no smoke, no water.

5. No fan, no pleats, no long parallel lines, nothing that radiates from one point. No round pits, no fields of craters.

Light: a low Sun in the south south east, soft long shadows falling toward the north north west, the same everywhere. A photograph from orbit: no horizon, no sky, no text, no border, no vignette.
