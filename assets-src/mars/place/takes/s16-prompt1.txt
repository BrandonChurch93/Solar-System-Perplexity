Task: repaint image 1 as one seamless nadir orbital photograph with the same framing, the same scale and north at the top, the whole picture in the light of its middle.

1. The middle stays as it is, every cliff and every light and dark patch where it is.

2. Around it every form stays exactly where it is and keeps its size and outline. Nothing is moved, added or taken away. Only the light and the colour change, to the light and colour of the middle, without a seam, a step, a frame or a change of sharpness or brightness at the border of the middle.

3. The Sun stands low in the upper left (north west). Faces that look toward the upper left are lit warm orange. Faces that look toward the lower right, and the ground behind cliffs, ridges and mesas, lie in long soft shadows that fall toward the lower right. Flat plateau tops are rust orange, not bright.

4. The canyon runs on to the upper right edge in shadow: its walls stepped, layered and dark, its floor very dark with the ground in it still to be made out, a thin warm haze over the floor.

5. Sharp natural detail everywhere, the same sharpness over the whole picture. No smear, no blur, no streaks, no etched lines, no bright outlines, no blue, no white, no pure black. No cloud, no water. No new craters.
