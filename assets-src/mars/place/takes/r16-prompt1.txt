The middle of image 1 (the square that takes the middle 44 percent of its width and height) is right and must stay exactly as it is: dark brown rocky high ground with cliffs, ledges and sunlit orange faces, and a deep shadowed canyon across its upper part.

Everything around that middle is only a rough sketch, right about where things are light and dark and wrong about what they look like. The left third is high plateau land west of the rocky ground, sunlit and orange; in the sketch it is smeared into soft blobs and shards with false blue, grey and white streaks and a pale bar at the upper left. Task: redraw image 1 as one seamless nadir orbital photograph with the same framing, the same scale and north at the top.
