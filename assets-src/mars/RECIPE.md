# Mars stand-ins

Stand-ins for the Phase 2 blockout. Each is replaced when the stop is built.

## public/assets/mars/full/standin-wrap.jpg and public/assets/mars/web/standin-wrap.jpg

- Tool: `python3 tools/assets/standin.py mars` (numpy and OpenCV, no model).
- Inputs: `references/approved/mars-hero.png`, `assets-src/mars/fit.json`.
- Prompt: none.
- What it does: unprojects the approved hero image onto the fitted sphere as an equirectangular map, 4096 wide for `full` and 2048 wide for `web`. The part of the globe the image does not show is filled by mirroring the visible part about a circle at 0.9 of the limb angle. A moon that sits in front of the disc is painted out first, because a stand-in sphere takes its place: a patch of the same globe from beside the moon is copied over its fitted circle with OpenCV seamless cloning. No other cleanup: the light, the limb glow, rings and shadows stay baked in.
- Take kept: the only one.
- Checks: the judged `hold` frame lines up with the approved hero image in `npm run compare`.

## public/assets/mars/full/standin-close.jpg and public/assets/mars/web/standin-close.jpg

- Tool: `python3 tools/assets/standin.py mars`.
- Input: `references/approved/mars-close.jpg`.
- Prompt: none.
- What it does: the approved close image, re-encoded at its own size for `full` and resized to 2048 wide for `web`. Nothing is redrawn.
- Take kept: the only one.
- Checks: the judged `terrain` frame lines up with the approved close image in `npm run compare`.

## public/assets/mars/{full,web}/country-8p5.bin, country-3p8.bin, country-1p6.bin, country-0p7.bin, country-0p3.bin and their -seen.png

- Files, every one written by `countrybake.py`: `public/assets/mars/full/country-8p5.bin`, `public/assets/mars/full/country-8p5-seen.png`, `public/assets/mars/full/country-3p8.bin`, `public/assets/mars/full/country-3p8-seen.png`, `public/assets/mars/full/country-1p6.bin`, `public/assets/mars/full/country-1p6-seen.png`, `public/assets/mars/full/country-0p7.bin`, `public/assets/mars/full/country-0p7-seen.png`, `public/assets/mars/full/country-0p3.bin`, `public/assets/mars/full/country-0p3-seen.png`, `public/assets/mars/web/country-8p5.bin`, `public/assets/mars/web/country-8p5-seen.png`, `public/assets/mars/web/country-3p8.bin`, `public/assets/mars/web/country-3p8-seen.png`, `public/assets/mars/web/country-1p6.bin`, `public/assets/mars/web/country-1p6-seen.png`, `public/assets/mars/web/country-0p7.bin`, `public/assets/mars/web/country-0p7-seen.png`, `public/assets/mars/web/country-0p3.bin`, `public/assets/mars/web/country-0p3-seen.png`.
- What they are: the heights of the country round the place, one square gnomonic plate per level about patches.spot, north up, the frame of the colour plates of the same width. `.bin`: raw little endian uint16, row major, row 0 at the north edge, no header; radii over the unit sphere = low + v / 65535 * (high - low), low and high per level in `src/stops/mars/country.json`. 2048 a side in full, 1024 in web (area scaled). `-seen.png`: 8 bit, one channel, 255 where the ground is the close view's own mesh (its heights as they are, except that no slope is steeper than `slopeMost`: a wall of the mesh is a slope centred on the wall).
- No picture is generated for them. Inputs: the distance files and alpha of the close view (`public/assets/mars/full/relief-L0-dist.bin`, `relief-L1-dist.bin`, `relief-L2-dist.bin`, `src/stops/mars/relief.json`), the frame of the close view on the globe (`out/work/place/geometry.json`, written by `npx tsx assets-src/mars/place/geometry.ts`), and the light in the plates named in `assets-src/mars/place/plates.json` (block country, levels, picture).
- Steps:
  1. `npx tsx assets-src/mars/place/geometry.ts`
  2. `PYTHONPATH=assets-src/mars/place python3 assets-src/mars/place/country.py` (calls `heights.py --picture-only` per level; then per level, coarse to fine: the canyon carried on along the trough of the globe's map with rims that wander by a function of the place (block canyon, wander), the coarser level, a membrane between seen ground and it, short waves read from the plate's light with the mesh plateau's roughness beside seen ground, made ground lowered behind what the picture's eye saw; beyond `far.fromDeg` from the eye's foot the mesh heights are smoothed, because from above they are ridged along the eye's rays, and take the relief read from the plate's light as the made ground does; last, `ground.hold_slope` holds every slope under `slopeMost`, 2.0 height over distance, the same at every level, because the cap of the globe draws a step between two texels as a curtain: steps of up to 87 texel widths stood in the first bake, 2.00 after). Every value: `plates.json`, block country.
  3. `python3 assets-src/mars/place/countrybake.py`
- Since the turn of the place (MAIL 461, MAIL 478; heights v9): the made ground is built for the bearing the plan names. The lines of the carried on canyon are given in `plates.json` (country.canyon.axis, canyon.east.axis) for the frame bearing (country.frame.bearingDeg, 105.936: the heading the image's canyon was first laid out for) and are turned with the place by `geom.canyon_lines`; from the end of the turned lines they run on by points given on the globe's map (axisMap). With the heading 245.94 the canyon of the image runs on 225.6 degrees: north east it thins out in the band of scarps of the map (canyon.fallFromDeg, fallToDeg), south west it runs as a side canyon into the main trough of the map and keeps its depth to its mouth. The main trough is made real in the heights (country.trough: its line, floor and walls measured on the 8.5 degree plate, which is the globe's own map at true places). Craters by code on the made plateau (country.craters, a seeded list, the same at every level). The membrane between seen and made ground is relaxed so that it meets the mesh at its border (no straight scarp). The taking of the made deep ground into the mesh's far heights is done after those are calmed. Run `country.py` twice after the bearing has moved: the first round has no turned plates to read relief from, the second reads it from `out/work/place/bake/land-*.png`.
