# Mars stand-ins

Stand-ins for the Phase 2 blockout. Each is replaced when the stop is built.

## public/assets/mars/full/standin-wrap.jpg and public/assets/mars/web/standin-wrap.jpg

- Tool: `python3 tools/assets/standin.py mars` (numpy and OpenCV, no model).
- Inputs: `references/approved/mars-hero.png`, `assets-src/mars/fit.json`.
- Prompt: none.
- What it does: unprojects the approved hero image onto the fitted sphere as an equirectangular map, 4096 wide for `full` and 2048 wide for `web`. The part of the globe the image does not show is filled by mirroring the visible part about a circle at 0.9 of the limb angle. A moon that sits in front of the disc is painted out first, because a stand-in sphere takes its place: a patch of the same globe from beside the moon is copied over its fitted circle with OpenCV seamless cloning. No other cleanup: the light, the limb glow, rings and shadows stay baked in.
- Take kept: the only one.
- Checks: the judged `hold` frame lines up with the approved hero image in `npm run compare`.

## public/assets/mars/full/standin-close.jpg and public/assets/mars/web/standin-close.jpg

- Tool: `python3 tools/assets/standin.py mars`.
- Input: `references/approved/mars-close.jpg`.
- Prompt: none.
- What it does: the approved close image, re-encoded at its own size for `full` and resized to 2048 wide for `web`. Nothing is redrawn.
- Take kept: the only one.
- Checks: the judged `terrain` frame lines up with the approved close image in `npm run compare`.

# The place under the dive (brief 238)

The ground on the globe under the place the dive flies to. All tools are in `assets-src/mars/place/`, their values in `assets-src/mars/place/plates.json`; the log is `assets-src/mars/PLACE-NOTES.md`. No third party picture is used: the inputs are the close view as the engine draws it at T and the patch levels of the globe.

- Chain: `sh assets-src/mars/place/dump.sh` (the close side at T as the engine draws it, and the twin cameras, `geometry.ts`), then `sh assets-src/mars/place/plates.sh lay force bake`.
  - lay: `dehaze.py` takes the air out of the close side picture; `layback.py --heights` lays it back from the dive twin as top down gnomonic plates of 3.8, 1.6, 0.7 and 0.3 degrees. Since the eleventh bake every texel of a plate is the ground at its true height (the heights of `country.py`, below, which runs first), so the picture's colour lies at its true place on a map from straight above and the cap of the globe can read the plate at the undisplaced direction; before, the picture was laid onto the smooth sphere and its cliffs were smeared over the plateau behind them. Alpha only where the picture's eye saw the ground.
  - force: every plate from 3.8 degrees down is the plate above, cropped, with its take nested (`nestbase.py`, tone matched at the rim and, by `nest.matchFloor` of `plates.json`, over the whole take), then held to the picture (`force.py`): where the twin sees the ground steeply the lay back is used as it is, where it stretches the take stands and only its broad tone is drawn to the picture (hue in full by `broadHue`, lightness by a gain between `broadLeast` and `broadMost`), its fine drawing damped (`broadDetail`, `broadColour`), fading with the stretch. Outside the fan the twin sees, the tone round the twin's foot follows the held near ground (`nearDeg`) and a darkening goes into the shaded ground first (`toneShade`). Since the tenth bake the ground outside the fan and near it (`grainFromDeg` to `grainToDeg`) takes the grain of the fan's own ground: the shade weight falls away there and the fine contrast of the take is brought to that of the fan at the nearest edge (`grainBlur`, `grainLeast`); and the fan's edge is drawn back from the straight edge of the picture's field along forms of the take (`edgeWander`).
  - paint (inside force, after each plate is held): `paint.py` lays colour by code on the canyon that `country.py` carries on west of the seen ground, where the takes show a plateau: the colour the close picture has on ground of the same height under the same light (a table of 16 heights by 8 cosines to the Sun measured on the seen ground of the 0.7 degree plate laid back at true places), with the fine drawing of the plate as grain; weight by the depth of the canyon function alone, nothing on ground the picture saw. Values: `plates.json`, block country, paint. No generated input.
  - bake: `bake.py` nests the plates from the deepest up, feathers and tone matches each to the plate above at its rim, bends highlights over, writes the JPEGs of both sets (94, 4:4:4, web at half size) and `src/stops/mars/levels.json`. The 8.5 degree plate is nested into new lit levels of 19 and 42 degrees made from the levels of main 03510ddd (albedo = look / light) and at half share into patch-wide.
  - Since the eighth bake the plates keep the hue of the globe's land: `plates.json` lead.paletteMix is 0 (the palette move of `bake.py` toned() is off) and the lit levels are baked with light = lead.light x `tint` of `src/stops/mars/near.json` (`geom.near_tint`). The change of hue to the picture's brown is made by the look by camera height (below), so no disc of another hue lies on the globe when the place is seen from above. `python3 assets-src/mars/place/topdown.py --levels src/stops/mars/levels.json --out FILE --east-deg 0.05 --high` shows the plates with the look above the near range, without `--high` with the look at T.
- Takes: image generation through the skill media (default route, square, 1254 px out), two input images each: the lead (image 1) and the close side picture at T (image 2, `out/work/place/gen/dive-picture.jpg` from dump.sh). ## src/stops/mars/near.json

- By hand against the film frame at T: the haze and the glow of the globe side close to the ground, compared in bands of the frame with the close side at frame 2454 (`out/work/place/bands.py`). No generated input. Since the seventh bake the ground haze is thinner (`hazeDay` 0.25, `hazeDepth` 0.8) and the ground glow stronger (1.6 1.12 0.72): with the older values the haze laid about 24 of 255 over the near ground at T where the picture has about 5 (measured on the globe alone at T, `npm run still -- --frame 2454 --tier draft --diag "beauty/side=orbit/t=81.808333/noplace"`, against the picture in the lower 480 rows, rows 380 to 600 and rows 300 to 380).
- The glow lobes of the ground haze and the shell (`glow`), aimed from the dive twin at T; the option is switched on in `set.ts`, so `haze.json` stays as on main.
- `ownGain`: the gain on the levels that carry their own light. `tintFollow` 1 makes them follow the descent tint as the lit ground does (measured with `python3 assets-src/mars/place/owngain.py` on a pair of renders of the same land as an own and as a lit level); `dive` and `rise` are tables of gain by camera height, all 1 for now.
- `tint` 0.666 1.383 2.56: the hue of the land by camera height. Measured in look values: the old land has 0.108 0.024 0.009 in the middle 3.8 degrees of the place (red over green 4.7; 3.3 at the rim of the 8.5 degree plate), the close side picture at T 0.0853 0.0459 0.0337 (1.86). The tint that brings the first to the hue of the second at equal luminance is 0.607 1.450 2.837; the value in use is 0.85 of that way. `set.ts` multiplies the tint of the lit levels by 1 + (tint - 1) x near and the own gain by (1 + (tint - 1) x near) / tint, with near the weight of the near look (0 above height 0.03, 1 below 0.0022: frames 2430 to 2446 and 2698 to 2714). The plates hold the look at T, so at T nothing changes against the seventh bake.

- What they are: the heights of the country round the place, one square gnomonic plate per level about patches.spot, north up, the frame of the colour plates of the same width. `.bin`: raw little endian uint16, row major, row 0 at the north edge, no header; radii over the unit sphere = low + v / 65535 * (high - low), low and high per level in `src/stops/mars/country.json`. 2048 a side in full, 1024 in web (area scaled). `-seen.png`: 8 bit, one channel, 255 where the ground is the close view's own mesh (its heights as they are, except that no slope is steeper than `slopeMost`: a wall of the mesh is a slope centred on the wall).
- No picture is generated for them. Inputs: the distance files and alpha of the close view (`public/assets/mars/full/relief-L0-dist.bin`, `relief-L1-dist.bin`, `relief-L2-dist.bin`, `src/stops/mars/relief.json`), the frame of the close view on the globe (`out/work/place/geometry.json`, written by `npx tsx assets-src/mars/place/geometry.ts`), and the light in the plates named in `assets-src/mars/place/plates.json` (block country, levels, picture).
- Steps:
  1. `npx tsx assets-src/mars/place/geometry.ts`
  2. `PYTHONPATH=assets-src/mars/place python3 assets-src/mars/place/country.py` (calls `heights.py --picture-only` per level; then per level, coarse to fine: the canyon carried on along the trough of the globe's map with rims that wander by a function of the place (block canyon, wander), the coarser level, a membrane between seen ground and it, short waves read from the plate's light with the mesh plateau's roughness beside seen ground, made ground lowered behind what the picture's eye saw; beyond `far.fromDeg` from the eye's foot the mesh heights are smoothed, because from above they are ridged along the eye's rays, and take the relief read from the plate's light as the made ground does; last, `ground.hold_slope` holds every slope under `slopeMost`, 2.0 height over distance, the same at every level, because the cap of the globe draws a step between two texels as a curtain: steps of up to 87 texel widths stood in the first bake, 2.00 after). Every value: `plates.json`, block country.
  3. `python3 assets-src/mars/place/countrybake.py`
