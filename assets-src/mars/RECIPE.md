# Mars stand-ins

Stand-ins for the Phase 2 blockout. Each is replaced when the stop is built.

## public/assets/mars/full/standin-wrap.jpg and public/assets/mars/web/standin-wrap.jpg

- Tool: `python3 tools/assets/standin.py mars` (numpy and OpenCV, no model).
- Inputs: `references/approved/mars-hero.png`, `assets-src/mars/fit.json`.
- Prompt: none.
- What it does: unprojects the approved hero image onto the fitted sphere as an equirectangular map, 4096 wide for `full` and 2048 wide for `web`. The part of the globe the image does not show is filled by mirroring the visible part about a circle at 0.9 of the limb angle. A moon that sits in front of the disc is painted out first, because a stand-in sphere takes its place: a patch of the same globe from beside the moon is copied over its fitted circle with OpenCV seamless cloning. No other cleanup: the light, the limb glow, rings and shadows stay baked in.
- Take kept: the only one.
- Checks: the judged `hold` frame lines up with the approved hero image in `npm run compare`.

## public/assets/mars/full/standin-close.jpg and public/assets/mars/web/standin-close.jpg

- Tool: `python3 tools/assets/standin.py mars`.
- Input: `references/approved/mars-close.jpg`.
- Prompt: none.
- What it does: the approved close image, re-encoded at its own size for `full` and resized to 2048 wide for `web`. Nothing is redrawn.
- Take kept: the only one.
- Checks: the judged `terrain` frame lines up with the approved close image in `npm run compare`.

### The sheet is cut where the surface is cut (25 November, MAIL 1104)

Why: inside one layer of the close set a near crest and the ground behind it were one sheet. A cell of the mesh across the step in the distance file is stretched as soon as the eye leaves the picture's eye, and the crest's outline runs along the cells and their diagonals: a sawtooth with a period of one cell and a depth of the parallax (draft frame 2668, x 675 to 740, y 630 to 700, and the mesa above it). A finer mesh does not cure it.

No picture is generated. Inputs: the distance files, colour and alpha of the close set as they stood before the cut, and the film's path.

1. The crests, by a script and not by eye. `python3 assets-src/mars/place/crests.py --poses POSES.json --kinds terrain --every 1 --out DIR` reads every pair of neighbour texels of each layer's distance file (the engine's ridge applied to its layer first), draws both through every eye of the path on a frame of 1080 lines and keeps the pairs whose parallax against each other is more than one cell of the mesh (motion.mesh) there. Pairs that a later layer hides from the picture's eye are left out. It writes `crests.json` (layer, box in picture pixels, largest parallax in px, the cell in px, frame) and `cut-<layer>.npy`. POSES.json holds eye, turn, field of view and the share of the set for each frame (a scratch file made from the stop's path; `--least` keeps only the eyes at which the set's share is over a value).
2. The cut (needs `pip install jpeglib`, 1.0.2 used). `python3 assets-src/mars/place/cutlayers.py --src SRC --out public/assets/mars` with SRC holding the layers before the cut for both sets, `relief.json` and the files of step 1. Per crest one cut layer C<n>: its colour is the picture in the box of its own alpha (on a grid of 4 px of its parent, so two mip levels agree with the parent's), kept without loss as PNG so that from the picture's eye it gives the parent's own pixels back; its distance is the crest's near side carried on across the step, times 1 minus cuts.bias; its alpha is whole on the near side and cuts.soft texels beside it, falls over one texel across the crest, and ends by a feather on the near side beyond the reach of the fill, where the cut layer and its parent lie in one surface again. The parent keeps its alpha; its distance behind the crest is the far side carried on under the crest over cuts.fill times the parallax and goes back to its own over cuts.band cells; its colour there is the nearest pixel of the far side, smoothed over a third of the parallax. So what the crest uncovers is ground of the same body from the far side: no hole, no second copy of the crest. From the picture's eye a cut layer covers exactly what it was cut from. What stood behind a parent stays behind it: the layers test depth against each other, so where a parent is pushed back and hides another layer (its own alpha whole, the other farther than the parent stood before), that layer's distance is at least the parent's new one times 1 plus cuts.behind, the need widened by one cell of that layer's grid.
