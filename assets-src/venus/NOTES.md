### Item 5, cloud detail (10:45 onward)

- Orange blocks on the bright cumulus edges (seen at 1185 and 1287 in native crops, the hero is cream there). Cause measured: the wrap stores albedo up to `TOP` 24 and scales brighter texels down with their hue kept. The sunlit cumulus tops on the night side need up to 135 (hero scene value 4.97 clipped in red, light curve there 0.042, 0.071, 0.108): stored 24, 6, 0.6 for a true 125, 31, 3.3. Five times too dim in scene light, and under the tone curve that is orange where the hero is nearly white. Not a JPEG fault (all maps are 4:4:4).
- Fix: the missing factor is stored. `wrap/lift.py` writes log2(true peak / 24) / 3 into the green channel of `wrap-flow.jpg` (red stays the flow layer; 0.019 percent of the map lifted, median 1.9, all with cap weight over 0.42, so nothing of the hero's limb glow). `globe.ts`: `lin *= exp2(max(g - floor, 0) * 3 * smoothstep(0.4, 0.8, texel peak))` (`look.lift`). The gate on the texel's own brightness keeps the soft lift map from brightening dark cloud beside a highlight in the patches. No new texture, no more memory.
- Black single pixels on the limb line at 1287 (draft): the globe mesh is a polygon (320 segments), its silhouette lies up to 4.8e-5 radii inside the sphere, the rim shader discards exactly inside the sphere: a gap of up to 0.026 px at 540 px radius, so about 2.6 percent of limb samples saw the sky between the two. Fix: the mesh is drawn 1 / cos(pi / seg) + 2e-4 larger and the globe shader discards rays that miss the unit sphere (same test as the rim, at the end of main so derivatives stay defined). The "dotted dark line" seen at review at -70 and -20 degrees was this, not the sharpen.
- Thin orange arcs near the grazing limb (vortex side, also 1287 upper right): streaks of the generated map in the outer 9 px, foreshortened to one pixel and kept sharp by anisotropic filtering. Fix: `look.graze = { most: 4, from: 0.32, to: 0.06 }`: the texture footprint of the wrap and the patches grows by up to 5 times as the view angle cosine falls from 0.32 to 0.06, as thick haze would do.
- Lightning: the painted bolt texture was read with an implicit derivative inside a branch (undefined mip level). Now `textureGrad` with the derivatives of the plane coordinate.
- Rise 1586 "combed, embossed strokes": the flow layer is drawn as veil(now) / veil(hold middle). By the rise the two are 12 degrees apart, so the map's own streaks are cancelled and a displaced copy laid over them: a double pattern. Fix in `set.ts`: after the terrain view the flow layer's clock is counted from the judged rise frame (`look.drift.riseAt` 0.75 of the rise), so the veil is neutral at 1586 and drifts around it. The globe is hidden by the covers and the terrain view in between, so nothing jumps in view. The band drift (the map's own shear) is unchanged and continuous.
