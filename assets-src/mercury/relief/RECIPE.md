# Mercury terrain relief recipe

All paths are repo paths. Scripts are in `assets-src/mercury/relief/` and run from the repo root with Python 3. Work files go to `out/work/mercury/relief/` (written `$R` below; not tracked). The terrain view is one relief layer: the approved terrain image as color, a distance for every pixel, an alpha cut at the horizon. Nothing on the ground moves.

## Outputs

| File | What |
|---|---|
| `public/assets/mercury/<set>/relief-color.jpg` | the approved terrain image with painted margins, full 8192 x 3864 (JPEG quality 93, 4:4:4), web 4096 x 1932 (quality 90) |
| `public/assets/mercury/<set>/relief-alpha.png` | 8 bit alpha, zero above the horizon, same sizes |
| `public/assets/mercury/<set>/relief-dist.bin` | distance from the camera in camera heights, raw little-endian float32, a quarter of the color size (full 2048 x 966, web 1024 x 483) |
| `public/assets/mercury/<set>/relief-masks.png` | 16 x 16 black: Mercury has no mist and no dust |
| `public/assets/mercury/<set>/relief-rim.png` | 2048 x 16, the warm glow the approved image carries on its horizon, as film codes on black (the same file in both sets) |
| `public/assets/mercury/<set>/relief-rim.bin` | the ribbon that carries it: 1025 columns x [top, bottom] x xyz, raw little-endian float32, in the relief's own frame and in camera heights |
| `src/stops/mercury/relief.json` | sizes, box of the canvas against the approved frame, field of view |
| `src/stops/mercury/rim.json` | file names and column count of the ribbon |

## Inputs

- `references/approved/mercury-close.jpg`, 2816 x 1584: the picture. Its pixels are kept; only margins are painted.
- No third-party input.

## Steps

1. Camera fit: `python3 assets-src/mercury/relief/camfit.py` fits a sphere horizon to 46 points of the approved image's horizon, with a vertical field of 60 degrees assumed. Writes `assets-src/mercury/relief/camera-fit.json`: roll -11.27 degrees, dip of the horizon 10.25 degrees, planet radius 61.7 camera heights, horizon 11.15 heights away, residual 0.022 degrees rms.
2. Block grid: `python3 assets-src/mercury/tools/deblock.py references/approved/mercury-close.jpg out/work/mercury/close-db.png` removes the 8 px block steps where the picture is calm (boundary step over inside step in the crater shadow 2.90 before, 0.78 after; sky 1.66 before, 0.97 after; mean change 0.39 codes). `python3 tools/ml/upscale.py --in out/work/mercury/close-db.png --out out/work/mercury/close-db-2x.png --width 5632` (Swin2SR, `tools/ml/MODELS.md`).
3. Margins. Canvas 4096 x 1932 at approved scale: 640 px to the left and to the right, 348 px below, nothing above (the sky is the film's). `python3 assets-src/mercury/relief/ext.py prep` writes the two squares `$R/left-in.jpg` and `$R/right-in.jpg` (1932 px, approved pixels with the missing part flat magenta). Each is painted with `generate_image`, model `nano_banana_pro`, aspect 1:1, one input image, the prompt below. Then `python3 assets-src/mercury/relief/ext.py prepbottom left=$R/left-t1.png right=$R/right-t1.png` writes `$R/bottom-in.jpg` (the gap below the approved frame, between the two squares), painted the same way at aspect 16:9. Prompt (`relief/prompts/margin.txt`):

```
Image 1 is part of a film frame: a view from low orbit over the cratered surface of the planet Mercury, lit by a hard low Sun from the upper left, with razor black shadows and no air. Part of the canvas is flat magenta: that part of the picture is missing.
Paint the missing part so that the picture continues past its edge as the same camera would see it: the same grey and bronze cratered plain, with craters, rims, small pits and ridges of the same sizes for their distance from the camera, the same hard light direction, the same long black shadows, the same grade, sharpness and film grain. Ground close to the camera at the bottom shows larger, sharper detail. Every feature that touches the magenta edge continues across it without a seam. Where the missing part reaches above the curved horizon it is black space with a few faint stars, and the horizon continues its curve.
No large new crater bigger than the ones beside it, no bright ray crater, no mountains, no haze, no glow on the ground. Every pixel outside the magenta area stays exactly as it is. No magenta left anywhere, no text, no border, no frame.
```

4. `python3 assets-src/mercury/relief/ext.py comp $R/ext-2816.png left=$R/left-t1.png right=$R/right-t1.png bottom=$R/bottom-t1.png`: each take is scaled to its box, checked by optical flow against the approved pixels it contains, matched to them in level, and blended in only where the canvas was magenta. Approved pixels are pasted back exactly.
5. Margins at twice the size: `python3 assets-src/mercury/relief/up.py cut` writes the three margin strips (64 px overlap into the approved frame), each is upscaled with `python3 tools/ml/upscale.py --in $R/strip-K.png --out $R/strip-K-2x.png --width W` (`W` twice the strip's width), `python3 assets-src/mercury/relief/up.py join` writes `$R/ext-2x.png` (8192 x 3864) with `out/work/mercury/close-db-2x.png` pasted over the approved frame.
6. Depth: `python3 tools/ml/depth.py --in $R/ext-2816.png --out-prefix $R/ext-depth` (Depth Anything V2, `tools/ml/MODELS.md`).
7. Lens fix: `python3 assets-src/mercury/relief/lens.py FILM --fresh` with frame 870 at review as `FILM` (twice). It measures approved over film on the ground in scene values and writes `$R/lens-fix.npy`, a smooth factor that `build.py` multiplies into the color, so the film's own bloom does not lift the shadows above the approved image (before the fix: ground 1.027, shadows 1.16). The factor is tidied before it is saved: its luminance is median filtered at a quarter of the size (11 px) and blurred 6 px (at 1920), its chroma blurred 40 px and held within 0.94 to 1.06, so it cannot draw dark rings or colored halos around the small glints of the approved image (`lens.py --tidy [FILE]` applies the same to an existing file). Round 7 used a narrow tidy (1.5 px), which left a dark dot of about 0.4 at every glint: the bright dots with dark rings in the crater shadows.
8. Build: `python3 assets-src/mercury/relief/build.py`. Before the lens fix the color is despeckled (`specks`): a pixel whose chroma is more than 5 codes off a 21 px median of the chroma takes the median chroma (fully from 8 codes), and a compact bright dot loses 70 percent of what it has above its surroundings; shadows are not touched. The approved image carries hundreds of 1 to 3 px colored sparkles on matte rock, which hard light and motion make read as noise. Distance = the sphere ground model of the camera fit times exp(detail); detail = the high-pass (300 px) of log(fitted depth) minus log(sphere), times the gain, faded out between 4.5 and 8 camera heights so the horizon stays one clean curve. Within 8 px of the horizon (`EDGE`, plus the horizon drop in the margins) the distance is the tangent line of the sphere's profile instead of its square root, continued 10 px above the horizon and constant from there: it is linear in the image, so the mesh carries it exactly and the horizon stays one clean curve when the camera leaves the fit pose (before, every mesh row that the tilted horizon crossed made a tooth of up to 8 px in the entry frames 744 to 755). `python3 assets-src/mercury/relief/build.py dist` writes only `relief-dist.bin` of both sets from `$R/relief-detail.npy`. The horizon of the ground is found per column of the color (`rim`: the strongest rise in brightness within 16 rows of the fitted horizon, centred on its ramp, median 5 and Gaussian 2 px along the columns; it lies 1.8 rows of 3864 under the fitted horizon in the median, -2.8 to 4.4). The alpha is cut on that line over two rows, and the color above it is the ground's own color held upward for 160 rows (`bleed`), so no mip level mixes sky into the edge. Until 22 Nov the alpha was cut 2.5 px (at 2816) above the fitted horizon and the 6 to 8 rows of dark red sky between the cut and the ground travelled with the relief: from every camera higher than the approved one they stood as a dark toothed line in front of the wide ground. Toward the canvas's left, right and lower borders the alpha falls to zero between 320 px (sides), 170 px (bottom) and 24 px of 4096 from the border (`FEATHER`), so the wide ground takes over without an edge. The default of `RELIEF_GAIN` is now 2.6. Writes both sets.

   The tight cut removes the thin warm line the approved image has on its horizon (it lay in the rows that were opaque before). `fringe` keeps it as its own piece: what the approved image has above the sky under the old cut (median of the six rows above it, calmed along the columns by a median of 31 and a Gaussian of 6 px), in scene values, weighted with the old alpha, for 14 rows above the detected edge and 2 below, median 15 and Gaussian 4 px along the columns, the rows under the edge holding the last free row. It is written as `relief-rim.png` and drawn by `src/stops/mercury/rim.ts` on a ribbon of 1024 segments that stands on the relief's far edge at the relief's own distance, added to the picture after the limb halo and before the wide ground, with no depth test and no alpha. So it shows only against the sky: at the approved camera the line is back (frame 870, horizon at the right: before the tight cut 38 28 24 and 82 70 67, without the ribbon 17 18 18 and 79 68 62, with it 32 19 19 and 93 72 65), and from any higher camera the far edge lies in front of the wide ground, which covers it (frames 744 and 989 are the same bytes with and without the ribbon at draft).

## Wide ground (Phase 6, item 69)

A second relief under the first, so that the terrain flight can leave the approved camera's cone: the flight goes from 1.44 heights behind and 2.18 heights up at frame 744, through the approved pose at 870, to 0.6 ahead and 2.14 up at 989. Drawn first, then the depth is wiped, then the main relief is drawn over it (`src/stops/mercury/set.ts`), so the approved pixels always win where they exist. Work files in `out/work/mercury/wide/` (`$W`).

| File | What |
|---|---|
| `public/assets/mercury/<set>/wide-color.jpg` | the main relief seen from the wide camera with painted ground round it, full 8192 x 4608 (quality 92, 4:4:4), web 4096 x 2304 (quality 90) |
| `public/assets/mercury/<set>/wide-alpha.png` | 8 bit alpha, a quarter of the color size: zero above the far edge of the ground and at the picture's borders |
| `public/assets/mercury/<set>/wide-dist.bin` | distance from the wide camera in camera heights, float32, a quarter of the color size |
| `src/stops/mercury/wide.json` | sizes, box, field of view, the wide camera's place and turn in terrain coordinates |

Wide camera (`wide-camera.json`): 0.6 heights behind the approved camera along the ground, 2.0 heights up, pitched 16 degrees down, focal 1540 px of 4096 x 2304, centre (2233, 1170). Every film ray of frames 744 to 989 that meets the planet lands inside its picture (`out/work/mercury/p6/cover.py`), with at least 1.39 texels of the full set per film pixel on 752 to 981.

1. `WIDE_SRC=0.6 python3 assets-src/mercury/relief/wide.py warp`: the main relief (color, alpha over 250, distances) splatted into the wide view, then `exact`: the distance of every known pixel by secant steps on the main relief's own surface (known 0.4297 of the picture, rms miss 0.00001). The splat's own distances were noisy and drew a dotted pattern and spikes.
2. `python3 assets-src/mercury/relief/wide.py prep`: `$W/paint-in.jpg`, the known part with the rest of the ground flat magenta and the sky black.
3. Paint: `nano_banana_pro`, 16:9, input `$W/paint-in.jpg`, prompt `assets-src/mercury/relief/prompts/wide.txt`. Upscale: `python3 tools/ml/upscale.py --in $W/paint-t2.png --out $W/paint-t2-2x.png --width 5504`.
4. `python3 assets-src/mercury/relief/wide.py comp $W/paint-t2.png $W/paint-t2-2x.png`: the take is registered to the known part by optical flow (median 2.79 px of 4096 before the fit, 0.59 after), levelled per channel (gains 0.973, 1.000, 0.983) and used only outside the known part; inside, the main relief's color.
5. Far band. The first paint left the strip between the far edge of the ground and the horizon black (37.9 rows of 2304 in the median, 118 at the right), which showed as a black wedge at the horizon of frames 744 to 750. `python3 assets-src/mercury/relief/wide.py farprep` writes two crops of 2304 x 1296 (`CROPS`: left at x 0, right at x 1792) with that band flat magenta (`$W/far-in-N.jpg`) or smeared (`$W/far-smear-N.jpg`). Paint: `nano_banana_pro`, 16:9, input `$W/far-in-1.jpg`, prompt `assets-src/mercury/relief/prompts/wide-far.txt`. Then `python3 assets-src/mercury/relief/wide.py farcomp 1 $W/far-t3-1.png` writes `$W/comp-far.png`: the right crop's band from the take (ground under the band 2.27 px of 2304 off in the median, moved by the median shift, levelled by 0.975, 1.000, 0.979, weight zero from 14 rows under the old far edge down, 400 px ramp at the crop's left side); everywhere else in the band the ground's own strip under the far edge, three times flattened (`FAR[4]`), which is how far ground reads at a grazing angle. The band left dark: 0.0001.
6. `python3 tools/ml/depth.py --in $W/comp-web.png --out-prefix $W/comp-depth --width 4096`, then `python3 assets-src/mercury/relief/wide.py build`: the depth is fitted to the planet's sphere (a 0.435, g 1.585, b 0.063, residual rms 0.171) and only its detail under 200 px is kept as relief (gain 2.6, clipped at 0.4, faded out from 4.5 to 8 heights and toward the picture's left, right and lower borders over 30 to 320 px); inside the known part the main relief's own distances, joined over 6 to 60 px. Beyond 8 heights the ground lies 0.12 heights under the sphere (`SINK`), so that at the approved pose it stays hidden behind the main relief's far edge (which sits up to 12.5 px under the sphere's horizon in the approved image) and comes up behind it only from higher cameras. The alpha ends at that lowered horizon and at the picture's left, right and lower borders.

Takes of the paint: `t1` rejected (tall boulders with long shadows, one large flat crater), `t2` kept (rolling plain with bowl craters). Far band: `t3` right crop kept (reaches the lowered horizon within -5 rows in the median, 11 at the 95th percentile); `t3` left crop and `t4` left crop (smeared input, prompt `prompts/wide-far-2.txt`) rejected: both took the band away and ended the ground at the old edge, so the left crop keeps the flattened strip. Four takes of six used.
