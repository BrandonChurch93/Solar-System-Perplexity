Venus, the whole planet in a dramatic low-key crescent-gibbous phase, wrapped in sculpted golden cloud bands with a glowing atmospheric limb, composed like a film poster.

THE LOOK: one frame from a top-budget science-fiction film, seen through a virtual camera flying through the solar system. Photoreal, physically based: real light, real materials, real depth, as if the viewer is there. Ultra-wide lens with an immense sense of scale. The Sun is the only key light: hard directional sunlight, a strong rim light, long shadows, deep contrast, with detail held in both the brightest and the darkest areas. Rich, saturated color pushed a little past reality without turning garish. Space is alive: a dense field of pin-sharp stars of varied brightness and tint, the dusty glowing band of the Milky Way crossing the background, faint nebular glow and drifting dust. Extreme fine detail at every scale, layered depth, volumetric haze where there is atmosphere. Natural lens bloom on the brightest highlights. It must not look like a spacecraft photograph, a textbook illustration, a painting or a stylized render, and nothing may look flat, plastic or plain. No text, labels, borders, watermarks, interface elements, spacecraft or people. 16:9 landscape.

FRAMING AND GEOMETRY: heroic whole-planet widescreen composition with Venus positioned left-of-center in the 16:9 frame and tilted slightly on its axis, viewed from a high-latitude flyby so both the sweeping equatorial V-chevrons and the bright swirling polar hood are prominent. Venus is a single, completely opaque, perfectly round planet with NO rings, NO ring shadows, and NO moons anywhere. Clean, sharp limb all around; zero stars visible inside the dark side of the disc. Keep the Sun off-frame to the left (slightly below horizontal centerline) with no Sun disc visible in frame.

THE GLOBE: unmistakably Venus: a rich, luminous world of dense sulfuric-acid clouds in warm gold, burnished amber, pale honey, and cream, organized into immense planetary-scale swirling streams and chevron bands wrapping around the globe. Raking sunlight from the left carves extraordinary multi-layered relief out of the vapour deck: overlapping cloud shelves, wind-torn silky streaks, scalloped wave fronts, and deep atmospheric canyons casting crisp, long shadows into the broad soft terminator. Everything is soft, volumetric gas and cloud—no crater-like pits, no hard spots, no rocky features. Along the sunlit left edge, the dense upper haze catches the light as an intense, glowing golden-white atmospheric rim arc.

NIGHT SIDE AND LIGHTNING: the right two-thirds of the globe lies in deep, atmospheric night shadow, subtly lifted from pure black by faint upper-haze twilight scatter. Deep within the dark nightside cloud deck, faint storm lightning flickers delicately in two small isolated clusters—tiny, soft warm-amber and pale-violet pulses diffused underneath miles of overcast clouds, never looking like city lights, surface lava, or jagged surface lines.

SKY, LIGHT AND CAMERA GRADE: single off-frame Sun to the left; every highlight, limb glow, and cloud shadow obeys this single light source. Across the expansive dark sky on the right, thousands of tiny pin-sharp stars and a delicate, dusky Milky Way band drift across deep space, held much dimmer than the sunlit golden limb so exposure feels genuinely photographic. Large-format anamorphic cinema look: a soft warm flare veil and subtle horizontal anamorphic streak coming directly off the brilliant left atmospheric rim, cool blue-black shadows against warm gold highlights, fine film grain, gentle vignette, sharp focus everywhere.
