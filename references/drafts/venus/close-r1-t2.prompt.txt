Visceral close orbital skim through towering golden cloud canyons on Venus, backlit by the blazing Sun perched low on the curved planetary limb.

THE LOOK: one frame from a top-budget science-fiction film, seen through a virtual camera flying through the solar system. Photoreal, physically based: real light, real materials, real depth, as if the viewer is there. Ultra-wide lens with an immense sense of scale. The Sun is the only key light: hard directional sunlight, a strong rim light, long shadows, deep contrast, with detail held in both the brightest and the darkest areas. Rich, saturated color pushed a little past reality without turning garish. Space is alive: a dense field of pin-sharp stars of varied brightness and tint, the dusty glowing band of the Milky Way crossing the background, faint nebular glow and drifting dust. Extreme fine detail at every scale, layered depth, volumetric haze where there is atmosphere. Natural lens bloom on the brightest highlights. It must not look like a spacecraft photograph, a textbook illustration, a painting or a stylized render, and nothing may look flat, plastic or plain. No text, labels, borders, watermarks, interface elements, spacecraft or people. 16:9 landscape.

FRAMING AND GEOMETRY: sweeping wide-lens flight frame positioned just above colossal, miles-deep canyons in Venus's sulfuric cloud tops, with the planet's immense curved horizon spanning the upper-middle 16:9 frame at a slight banking roll (~8 degrees) for visceral flight momentum. NO rings, NO moons, NO solid terrain or rock anywhere. Below the curved horizon is 100% opaque layered atmosphere; above the atmospheric glow is open deep space. Tack-sharp focus from nearest cloud tendrils to distant stars—no bokeh, no motion blur, no floating particles.

CLOUD CANYONS AND LAYERED HAZE: an awe-inspiring labyrinth of towering golden, burnished amber, champagne, and smoky bronze cloud walls carved by super-rotating jet streams into vast terraced canyons and abyssal vapour troughs. Above and between the towering convective crests hang multiple paper-thin, distinct horizontal layers of glowing golden sulfuric haze. Every structure is soft, fibrous, wind-sculpted atmospheric vapour—never solid stone, craters, or plastic blobs.

ONE SUN AND BACKLIT PHYSICS: the blazing white-gold Sun sits right at the curved atmospheric horizon slightly left of center, partially veiled by thin upper golden haze. Strictly obey single-source backlighting: all cloud walls between the Sun and the camera cast long dark shadows toward the viewer, keeping their camera-facing flanks in rich, cool-toned deep shadow while igniting their upper rims and translucent edges in fiery molten gold, and sending long, dramatic volumetric shafts of sunlight fanning through breaches in the cloud walls.

SKY AND CAMERA GRADE: above the brilliant golden-orange limb band, the sky transitions into dark inner-solar-system space across the upper frame and right side, sprinkled with delicate pin-sharp stars and a faint, subtle Milky Way band far dimmer than the sunlit haze. Photographed on large-format anamorphic film: authentic optical bloom and a single horizontal anamorphic streak anchored directly to the blazing Sun on the horizon, low-key exposure with vast shadow depth, fine film grain, subtle vignette.
