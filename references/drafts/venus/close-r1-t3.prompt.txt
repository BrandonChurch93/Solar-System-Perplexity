High-speed orbital pass sweeping diagonally over endless terraced golden cloud canyons on Venus, with the blazing Sun grazing the far right curved horizon.

THE LOOK: one frame from a top-budget science-fiction film, seen through a virtual camera flying through the solar system. Photoreal, physically based: real light, real materials, real depth, as if the viewer is there. Ultra-wide lens with an immense sense of scale. The Sun is the only key light: hard directional sunlight, a strong rim light, long shadows, deep contrast, with detail held in both the brightest and the darkest areas. Rich, saturated color pushed a little past reality without turning garish. Space is alive: a dense field of pin-sharp stars of varied brightness and tint, the dusty glowing band of the Milky Way crossing the background, faint nebular glow and drifting dust. Extreme fine detail at every scale, layered depth, volumetric haze where there is atmosphere. Natural lens bloom on the brightest highlights. It must not look like a spacecraft photograph, a textbook illustration, a painting or a stylized render, and nothing may look flat, plastic or plain. No text, labels, borders, watermarks, interface elements, spacecraft or people. 16:9 landscape.

FRAMING AND GEOMETRY: panoramic 16:9 wide-angle cinema frame from a higher orbital-skim altitude above Venus, where the planet's sweeping curved limb arches diagonally from mid-left down toward the lower-right horizon, leaving a generous expanse of star-filled deep space across the upper-left sky. NO rings, NO ring arcs, NO moons, NO rocky surface. Everything below the crisp atmospheric limb is opaque cloud deck; everything from near cloud ridges to distant stars is in sharp focus with zero depth-of-field blur.

CLOUD CANYONS AND LAYERED HAZE: stretching for thousands of kilometers below the camera is Venus's super-rotating cloud sea, sculpted into parallel ranges of towering golden cloud canyons, folded wind-sheared storm walls, and braided chevron ridges in hues of molten gold, pale champagne, honey, ochre, and deep umber shadow. Three delicate, distinct horizontal decks of luminous golden-amber upper haze float above the storm tops and curve smoothly along the planetary limb. Pure atmospheric vapour at massive planetary scale—no craters, no dimples, no Earth land or ocean.

ONE SUN AND GRAZING SIDE-BACKLIGHT: on the far right where the curved horizon meets space, the low Sun blazes intensely just above the limb. Every shadow and highlight across the entire cloudscape strictly obeys that single low right-hand Sun: long, dramatic raking shadows stretch from right to left across deep cloud canyons, sunlit right-facing vapour ridges gleam in brilliant gold-white, left-facing canyon walls sit in deep cool-bronze shadow, and long golden shafts of sunlight slice nearly horizontally from right to left through gaps in the cloud ridges and across the layered haze.

SKY AND CAMERA GRADE: away from the blazing right-horizon Sun, the upper-left cosmos is deep black with thousands of tiny pin-sharp stars and the delicate dusty band of the Milky Way, exposed naturally for the bright sunlit horizon so the sky remains restrained and believable. Large-format anamorphic cinema look: warm solar bloom and a clean horizontal anamorphic flare streak extending solely from the blazing right-horizon Sun, cool dark shadows against warm golden highlights, fine grain, gentle vignette.
