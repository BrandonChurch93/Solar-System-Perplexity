THE SUNSPOT: in the right foreground, the near rim of an enormous sunspot drops away beneath the camera like a canyon, its floor a deep near-black umbra, its walls a ring of long, fibrous, glowing penumbral filaments in bronze, amber and orange streaming down into the dark, fine as combed metal, each filament catching the glow of the surface. 

THE SURFACE: beyond and around it, a boiling molten sea of convection cells, domes of incandescent gold and tangerine plasma separated by darker red-brown lanes, receding in perspective to a hazy horizon, the brightness held so every cell is readable and never blown out.

OVERHEAD: one gigantic plasma loop rises from the far side of the sunspot and arches high over the frame, silhouetted at its peak against black space, made of many braided glowing strands of orange and magenta-rose plasma with bright beads of coronal rain falling along its legs. Smaller loops and spicules fringe the horizon like a burning forest. A faint field of pin-sharp stars in the black above, far dimmer than the plasma.

LIGHT AND CAMERA: the glowing surface lights everything from below, the loop back-lit against space. Everything in sharp focus: no depth-of-field blur, no bokeh, no floating debris.
