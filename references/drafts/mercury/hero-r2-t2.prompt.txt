Whole airless Mercury swings past the virtual camera like a dangerous dark, heavy presence, with the glaring Sun behind it. Single 16:9 frame.

THE LOOK: one frame from a top-budget science-fiction film, seen through a virtual camera flying through the solar system. Photoreal, physically based: real light, real materials, real depth, as if the viewer is there. Ultra-wide lens with an immense sense of scale. The Sun is the only key light: hard directional sunlight, a strong rim light, long shadows, deep contrast, with detail held in both the brightest and the darkest areas. Rich, saturated color pushed a little past reality without turning garish. Space is alive: a dense field of pin-sharp stars of varied brightness and tint, the dusty glowing band of the Milky Way crossing the background, faint nebular glow and drifting dust. Extreme fine detail at every scale, layered depth, volumetric haze where there is atmosphere. Natural lens bloom on the brightest highlights. It must not look like a spacecraft photograph, a textbook illustration, a painting or a stylized render, and nothing may look flat, plastic or plain. No text, labels, borders, watermarks, interface elements, spacecraft or people. 16:9 landscape.

Gritty charcoal, cool neutral grey, bronze mineral plains, no blue or orange coating. The planet is unambiguously airless. A hard clean opaque rocky limb, no illuminated layer above it, no photographically pasted rim. The Sun is a distinct very large blazing white-gold disk cropped by the image edge, composed as a separate silhouette with ragged hints of solar granulation, not a flat white shape and never behind a translucent globe. Shape of the brightness gradient gently like real exponential veiling glare, keep it concentrated at the solar edge.

SKY AND GRADE: a low-key large-format high-dynamic-range exposure, strongly directional, full-silhouette blacks; all surrounding empty space is deep black, no faint wisps or bright Milky Way near the Sun. On the far shadow side the dusty Milky Way is barely visible, with restrained very fine sharp stars utterly subordinate to the sunlit crescent. No stars in any part of the opaque disc, including shadows. Fine film grain and subtle natural halation only on the Sun. No flying specs, moons, stray flare streaks or Saturn motifs. No atmospheric haze on Mercury.
