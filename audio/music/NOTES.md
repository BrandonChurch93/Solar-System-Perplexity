# Music for the film: notes

Three instrumental tracks made to fit the film. The film is finished work and nothing in it was changed for the music: no file under `src/`, `public/`, `config/`, `cut/` or `out/` is touched by this folder.

Every number below carries one of three labels. MEASURED: computed from the files in this folder by the scripts in `src/`. STATED: said by a tool or a service and not checked here. UNKNOWN: not established.

Why take 3 of each. Take 1 used 29 sections that all follow their neighbours closely; the result ran evenly loud from start to end. Take 2 put the energy words first, took out the words for silence and fading, and chose for each section how closely it follows its neighbours. Take 3 frees every section after the first from its neighbours, so each arrival can hit and each surface can thin out at its own second, and runs 3.5 s past the film so that the generator's own ending falls after 180 s and is cut. In the orchestral direction takes 1 and 2 were also flagged for a wordless choir by a listening check (STATED, see "Vocals"); take 3 was not flagged in that way on its excerpts.

No take follows the plan's levels on its own. MEASURED on the raw takes with `src/measure.py`: none of the nine raw takes lifts by 3 dB at all eight arrivals, none drops by 3 dB in all eight surface views, and none has its loudest six seconds at the ending. That is why the kept takes are fitted (next section).

## The fit: what was done to the kept takes

`src/fit.py` does four things and nothing else: it cuts the take to exactly 180.000 s; it multiplies it by one slow volume curve whose points sit on the film's timeline; it lays the film's fade (177.5 s to 180.0 s) on it; it sets the peak to 1 dB under full scale. No note is moved, stretched, repeated or replaced, and no other sound is mixed in. The lift at each arrival, the drop in each surface view and the swell at the ending that you hear in the fitted tracks come in large part from this volume curve, not from the playing alone. The raw takes are beside them for comparison.

The curve holds one level through each arrival (5 s) and each surface view (6 s) and ramps between them over the 3 s of each dive; before each landing the level sits a little lower and rises into the landing over 0.4 s.

## Known weak points

- Orchestral: the first 1.06 s are silent (MEASURED: the first half second is digital silence). The plan asks for sound from the first frame; this take does not give it, and the fit does not add sound.
- Ambient: the first sound over -50 dBFS comes at 0.28 s; the first half second sits at -59.1 dB, just above the floor of the check.
- Hybrid: a thin passage at about 15 to 18 s, in the travel to Mercury, where the take almost stops before the first arrival.
- All three: the dynamics are helped by the volume curve (see "The fit"). In the orchestral and hybrid tracks the music does not always change at the second of a landing; the level does.
- The cue sheet of the film (`cut/CUES.md`) asks for sound at full level on frame 0. The music task asks for a quiet opening at the Sun; the task is newer and holds. The opening is quiet.

## Vocals

The task says no vocals. Every section of every plan carries the negative words "vocals", "singing", "choir", "lyrics" and "spoken word".

- MEASURED: the generator's own stem separation was run on the three kept takes (`src/stems.sh`), and the level of each stem measured in 5 s windows (`src/stemlevels.py`). The voice stem as a whole sits at -96.1 dBFS (orchestral), -120.0 dBFS (ambient) and -89.9 dBFS (hybrid), and at least 61 dB under the whole take in every window: the separation finds no voice in any kept take.
- STATED, by a listening model that proved unreliable (it gave the same file 10 of 10 and 1 of 10 on one question): orchestral take 3, thirteen checks on excerpts heard no voice, and two checks on the whole file named a wordless choir, at different times (84 to 95 s, 109 to 124 s). Hybrid take 3: for the excerpt 60 to 90 s four checks heard no voice and three named a choir; no other excerpt was flagged. Ambient take 3: no voice named.
- UNKNOWN: whether a listener hears a choir like pad in the orchestral track near 84 to 124 s or in the hybrid track near 60 to 90 s. A separation that finds no voice stem does not rule out a string or synth pad that sounds like one. Listen to those two places before picking.
